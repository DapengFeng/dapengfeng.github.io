The investment return from an excellent business still depends on the purchase price. The operating assets produce cash; the buyer chooses how much to pay for access to that cash. A high return on the company’s capital does not ensure a high return for the investor who buys it.即使是一门优秀的生意，投资回报也取决于买入价格。经营资产产生现金，买家则决定愿意为取得这些现金流支付多少。公司自身的资本回报率很高，并不保证买入这家公司的投资者也能获得很高的回报。

Case · See’s operating return was not the buyer’s return真实案例：喜诗的经营回报，不等于买家的回报

Buffett’s 2014 letter, printed p. 27, recalls roughly $4 million of annual pre-tax profit on $8 million of net tangible assets at See’s in 1972, and a purchase price of $25 million. Dividing the same profit figure by different amounts answers different questions.巴菲特在 2014 年股东信第 27 页回顾：1972 年，喜诗每年的税前利润约为 400 万美元，净有形资产约为 800 万美元，而收购价格为 2,500 万美元。同一个利润数字，可以用两种不同的分母来衡量。

The purchase price exceeded the book value of See’s net tangible assets. The gap reflects a judgment about future earnings and their durability. The retrospective explains why Munger emphasized the business’s quality; the successful outcome does not mean every premium over book value is justified. To estimate value before a purchase, we need to examine the evidence available at the time. Later success does not remove the uncertainty the buyer originally faced.这次收购的价格高于喜诗的净有形资产账面价值。两者之间的差额，反映了买家对未来盈利及其持续性的判断。这段回顾说明了芒格为何强调生意质量；收购后来取得成功，不代表任何高于账面价值的收购价都合理。买入前估值，需要检查当时能够获得的证据。不能因为后来成功，就忽略买家当初面对的不确定性。

A good business still has a purchase price生意好不好，与买得贵不贵是两个问题

Financial analysis estimates earning power; valuation asks what that future cash is worth today. The 1992 letter explains value through discounted future cash and emphasizes the difficulty of estimating those flows. A high ROIC does not tell us whether the current share price is reasonable. High expectations for that earning power may already be reflected in the price.财务分析帮助判断持续赚钱的能力；估值则追问，未来的现金流在今天值多少钱。1992 年股东信用未来现金折现来说明价值，也强调估计现金流本身的困难。很高的 ROIC 本身不能说明当前股价是否合理；股价中可能已经包含了对这种盈利能力的很高预期。

Higher EPS does not prove that a buyback was sensible每股收益提高，也不能证明回购划算

The 2011 letter's “Share Repurchases” section pairs two conditions: sufficient funds for operations and liquidity, and a repurchase price below conservatively estimated business value. The 2022 letter again distinguishes an increased ownership percentage from the harm caused by overpaying. A lower share count is only one side of the calculation: the company has also paid out cash.2011 年股东信“股份回购”一节提出了两个需要同时满足的条件：公司有足够资金满足经营和流动性需求，且回购价格低于保守估计的企业价值。2022 年股东信又明确区分了持股比例上升与高价回购造成的损害。股数减少只是需要考虑的一方面；回购支出也减少了公司的现金。

Assume a debt-free business has operating value 400 and genuinely excess cash 100, giving total equity value 500 across 100 shares: value per share 5. Its annual profit is 40. It spends all 100 of excess cash on a buyback. To isolate the mechanism, assume the cash earned no interest, operations are unchanged, and there are no fees, taxes, new shares or market reactions. “Value” here is a supplied assumption, not something the accounts can establish exactly.假设一家无债企业的经营价值为 400，另有无需用于经营或偿债的多余现金 100，总股权价值为 500。公司共有 100 股，每股价值为 5，年度利润为 40。它将这笔现金（100）全部用于回购股票。为了单独观察回购的影响，假设这笔现金原本不产生利息收入，企业经营保持不变，回购没有额外费用或税款，也没有新增股份或市场反应。这里的“价值”是给定的假设，不是财报能够精确证明的数字。

Compare a feasible alternative与真正可行的替代选择比较

An initial cash yield is only a starting point for comparison. Compare currency, tax treatment, leverage, liquidity, duration and uncertainty on a consistent basis. A highly uncertain 10% return is not automatically better than a more dependable 8%. If the estimated cash flows and the purchase price do not support a defensible margin for error, waiting can be rational.初始现金收益率只能作为比较的起点。先确认币种、税费、杠杆、流动性、期限和不确定性有哪些差异，再在可比的条件下判断。高度不确定的 10% 收益率，并不一定优于较可靠的 8%。如果现金流估计与买入价格都无法支持一个可靠的安全边际，等待也可以是合理选择。

Practice · If the price falls, must the margin of safety increase?练习：价格跌了，安全边际一定扩大吗？

No. If the cash the business can sustainably generate also falls, estimated value can fall faster than price. Re-estimate future cash flow first, then compare the new price with that value range. A discount to a former stock price is not a margin of safety.不一定。如果公司持续产生现金的能力也在下降，估值可能比股价下跌得更快。应先重新估计未来现金流，再将新价格与估值范围比较。相对于过去股价的折扣，并不等于安全边际。
