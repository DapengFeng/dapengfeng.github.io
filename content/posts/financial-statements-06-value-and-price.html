Case · See’s operating return was not the buyer’s return真实案例：喜诗的经营回报，不等于买家的回报

Buffett’s 2014 letter, printed p. 27, recalls roughly $4 million of annual pre-tax profit on $8 million of net tangible assets at See’s in 1972, and a $25 million purchase. The same profit has two different denominators.巴菲特在 2014 年股东信第 27 页回顾：1972 年喜诗年税前利润约 400 万美元、净有形资产约 800 万美元，收购花了 2,500 万美元。同一份利润，对应两种分母。

A buyer could not purchase the business for its book asset amount. The gap reflects a judgment about future earnings and durability. The retrospective explains why Munger emphasized the business’s quality; its successful outcome does not mean every premium over book value is justified. Our job is to examine the evidence available for a prospective estimate, rather than let the later success erase the original uncertainty.买家不能按账面资产金额直接买下这门生意。两者之间的差额，包含对未来盈利和持续性的判断。回顾材料解释了芒格为何强调生意质量；后来成功，不代表任何账面溢价都合理。我们要检查作出估计时的依据，不能让事后结果抹去当时的不确定性。

It may already be reflected in a very demanding stock price.财务分析帮助判断持续赚钱的能力；估值则追问，那些未来现金今天值多少钱。1992 年股东信用未来现金折现来说明价值，也强调估计现金流本身的困难。ROIC 很高，并没有回答价格问题；这种优点可能已经反映在要求极高的股价里。

Do not add perpetual growth while keeping every dollar available for distribution if achieving that growth needs additional capital. Higher EPS does not prove that a buyback was sensible每股收益提高，也不能证明回购划算

The 2011 letter's “Share Repurchases” section pairs two conditions: sufficient funds for operations and liquidity, and a repurchase price below conservatively estimated business value. The 2022 letter again distinguishes an increased ownership percentage from the harm caused by overpaying. Fewer shares is only half the calculation; the cash spent has also left the company.2011 年股东信“股份回购”一节把两个条件放在一起：经营与流动性所需资金充足，以及回购价格低于保守估计的企业价值。2022 年股东信又明确区分了持股比例上升与高价回购造成的损害。股数减少只是计算的一半，付出去的现金也已经离开公司。

Assume a debt-free business has operating value 400 and genuinely excess cash 100, giving total equity value 500 across 100 shares: value per share 5. Its annual profit is 40. It spends all 100 of excess cash on a buyback. To isolate the mechanism, assume the cash earned no interest, operations are unchanged, and there are no fees, taxes, new shares or market reactions. “Value” here is a supplied assumption, not something the accounts can establish exactly.假设一家无债企业的经营价值为 400，另有真正多余的现金 100，总股权价值 500，共 100 股，每股价值为 5，年度利润 40。它拿这 100 现金全部回购。为了单独观察机制，假设现金原来没有利息收入，经营不变，没有费用、税收、新增股份或市场反应。这里的“价值”是给定假设，不是财报能够精确证明的数字。

Compare a feasible alternative与真正可行的替代选择比较

An initial cash yield is only a starting comparison. Put currency, tax, leverage, liquidity, duration and uncertainty on comparable terms. A speculative 10% is not automatically better than a more dependable 8%. Waiting can be rational when neither the cash estimate nor the price offers a defensible margin for error.初始现金收益率只能作为比较起点。币种、税费、杠杆、流动性、期限和不确定性要先对齐。高度不确定的 10%，不自动优于较可靠的 8%。现金估计或价格都没有留下有依据的误差空间时，等待也可以是合理选择。
