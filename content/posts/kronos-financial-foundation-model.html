What does Kronos contribute to a trading strategy?Kronos 能为交易策略增加什么？

A model forecasts a higher price. Before that becomes a trade, a quantitative researcher needs to answer three questions: does the forecast add information, can an order capture the predicted move, and is the remaining return worth the risk? This is the financial research problem around Kronos. The model is a forecasting component; signal construction, portfolio allocation and execution remain separate research decisions.模型预测价格会上涨。在把它变成一笔交易之前，量化研究人员还要回答三个问题：预测是否带来增量信息，订单能否捕捉这段变化，剩下的收益是否值得承担风险？这才是围绕 Kronos 的金融研究问题。模型承担预测，信号构造、组合配置与交易执行还需要各自的研究。

Define the target before evaluating the forecast先定义收益目标，再评价预测

Financial quantitative research starts with an observable hypothesis. For stock selection, the question may be whether a score ranks future returns across tradable stocks at the same decision time. For timing, it is whether a signal predicts one instrument’s return across successive decisions. For risk forecasting, the target may instead be realized variance or a loss event. These tasks use different labels and evaluation axes, even when they share the same price forecast.金融量化研究从一个可检验的假设开始。做选股，关心的是同一决策时刻，评分能否把可交易股票的未来收益排对；做择时，关心的是一个标的在不同时刻的收益能否被预测；做风险预测，目标可能是实现方差或亏损事件。三者即使共用一组价格预测，也需要不同的标签与评价轴。

A factor is a measurable score assigned to an asset at a decision time. Its value is established by a relationship with later outcomes, not by whether it came from a formula or a neural network. For stock selection, calculate the Spearman correlation across the stocks available at each time t, then examine the time series of those correlations.因子可以理解为在某个决策时刻赋给资产的可计算评分。它的价值来自与后续结果的关系，而不是来自手工公式还是神经网络。做选股时，应在每个 t 时刻，对当时可交易的股票计算评分与未来收益的 Spearman 秩相关，再研究这条相关系数时间序列。

A positive average IC indicates positive association in the evaluated sample; its uncertainty and stability still need testing. It is not a net profit measure. A point forecast cannot summarize risk点预测不能概括风险

The choice of loss already determines which forecast is preferred. Consider a last close of 100 and two equally likely terminal prices, 106 and 94. Squared-error risk is 36 when predicting 100 and 72 when predicting 106. The conditional mean is optimal for that loss, although it conceals the dispersion between the two possible outcomes. A point forecast and a risk forecast therefore need different summaries of the same distribution.损失函数已经决定了模型倾向于输出什么。设最后收盘价为 100，两个等概率终点为 106 和 94；预测 100 的平方误差期望为 36，预测 106 则为 72。条件均值在这个损失下最优，却没有表达两个结果之间的离散程度。因此，点预测与风险预测需要从同一分布提取不同统计量。

A conditional distribution preserves alternatives until a payoff or risk statistic is selected. Kronos represents that distribution through discrete states and next-token generation. If the only target is return or realized volatility, directly predicting that label is a valid baseline. Continuous probabilistic forecasters are also relevant controls. The research hypothesis is that Kronos’s learned representation improves forecast quality under a given computation budget. Multimodal futures alone do not establish that tokenization is necessary.条件分布允许在定义收益或风险统计量之前保留不同未来。Kronos 用离散状态和下一 token 生成来表示这一分布；如果只研究收益或实现波动率，直接预测相应标签也是合理基线；连续概率预测器同样可作为对照。待检验的假设是：在给定计算预算下，学到的表示是否改善预测质量。未来具有多种可能，本身不能证明 token 化必不可少。

The public Kronos predictor averages decoded paths when sample_count exceeds one. Its returned DataFrame is therefore a point forecast. To study variance, tail losses or coverage, preserve the individual paths before this averaging and calculate the chosen statistic on each path. Sample dispersion describes the model’s distribution; whether it matches observed uncertainty requires held-out calibration tests.公开 Kronos 预测接口在 sample_count 大于 1 时，会对解码路径求平均，返回的 DataFrame 因而是点预测。研究方差、尾部亏损或区间覆盖率，需要在这一步平均之前保留单条路径，再逐条计算统计量。采样离散程度描述的是模型自身的分布，是否符合实际不确定性，还需要留出数据上的校准检验。

The trading layer converts a forecast distribution into an exposure decision. Define the payoff under the chosen entry and exit rules, compute it on each retained path, and estimate its mean, dispersion and adverse outcomes. Position construction then adds risk limits, liquidity and implementation costs. The portfolio optimization literature treats forecasts and allocation as separate components; Kronos supplies the former and does not by itself specify an executable strategy.交易层把预测分布转换为敞口决策。先按既定入场和退出规则定义收益，再对保留的每条路径计算收益，估计均值、离散程度和不利结果；仓位构造进一步加入风险上限、流动性与实施成本。组合优化研究将预测与配置作为不同组件；Kronos 提供前者，其本身没有完整规定可执行的策略。

This also explains why numerical quantization will matter later. Reconstruction asks whether the observed bar survives compression; forecasting asks whether its code helps predict unseen outcomes; trading asks whether the resulting decision remains useful after risk, costs and execution. A deterministic compression cannot manufacture future information absent from its input, though it can make useful structure easier for a finite model to learn. If two histories with different conditional returns are collapsed into indistinguishable codes and available side information, that distinction is lost to the predictor. Out-of-sample tests and incremental value样本外检验与增量价值

The first experiment should isolate incremental value at a fixed information set. Freeze the instrument universe, bar frequency, forecast origin, target, holding rule and available training data. Evaluate a matched simple baseline and Kronos on identical dates. For an existing signal, also test a validation-selected combination on untouched test periods: a standalone forecasting improvement does not establish that it adds information to the current research stack. The financial task now has a target, a decision rule and a test. The next question is whether the model’s representation preserves the information that task needs. One input row is [open, high, low, close, volume, amount]. OHLC are prices within the same interval; volume measures traded units and amount measures traded value. A completed bar such as [100, 103, 99, 102, 2000, 202000] is only a numerical example. In KronosPredictor.predict, each feature gets its own history mean and population standard deviation. Joint encoding imposes a hypothesis about the information set: price shape and trading activity may be more useful together than as independent marginal features. Numerical quantization: what the representation loses数值量化：表示舍弃了什么

Quantization merges nearby representations into a finite set of states. Its immediate consequence is many-to-one reconstruction: an encoder stores a region ID, and the decoder returns a representative value. Within-region differences are lost. They should not be called noise merely because they are compressed; whether they matter for future returns is a downstream empirical question. A scalar example in [0, 2) makes the capacity/error trade-off explicit.数值量化把表示合并为有限状态，直接后果是多对一重建：编码器保存区域编号，解码器返回代表值，区域内部的差异被舍弃。不能因为这些差异被压缩，就把它们认定为噪声；它们是否包含未来收益信息，要由下游实验判断。先用 [0, 2) 上的标量例子明确容量与误差的权衡。

With step Δ = 0.5 there are four cells, requiring two fixed-length bits. The cell [1, 1.5) has ID 2 and midpoint 1.25: both 1.1 and 1.4 decode to 1.25. A half-width step gives eight cells and needs three bits. Now 1.1 and 1.4 have different IDs, with reconstructions 1.125 and 1.375.步长 Δ = 0.5 时，一共有四个区域，需要 2 bit 定长编号。其中 [1, 1.5) 的编号为 2，中点为 1.25：1.1 和 1.4 都会还原成 1.25。把步长减半，区域数变成八个，需要 3 bit；此时 1.1 和 1.4 分属不同区域，分别还原成 1.125 和 1.375。

The error bound applies inside these covered cells; saturation outside the covered range needs a separate error analysis. The interval ID is a label, while its representative is a numerical value. For instance, ID 2 in this example decodes to 1.25, not to the number 2.这个误差界适用于所覆盖的区域；超出范围后若采用饱和截断，需要另算误差。区域编号是标签，代表值才是恢复的数值。例如本例的编号 2 解码为 1.25，不能直接当作数值 2 使用。

The trade-off is between coding rate and distortion. For a fixed-length K-bit representation, the nominal rate is K bits and at most 2ᴷ IDs are available; distortion measures what reconstruction loses. The scalar example has a known step size, so its error is easy to calculate. A learned tokenizer instead shapes both the partition and the reconstruction through training. More bits increase nominal capacity, but do not guarantee a better trained predictor or better returns.这就是编码率与失真的权衡。K bit 定长表示的名义编码率为 K bit，最多有 2ᴷ 个编号；失真衡量重建丢掉了多少信息。标量例子的步长已知，误差容易计算；可学习 tokenizer 则通过训练调整划分与重建方式。更多 bit 增加名义容量，但不保证训练后的预测器更好，更不保证交易收益更高。

In the previous normalization example, standard deviation 2 turns a normalized error of 0.15 into an original-unit error of approximately 0.30. A decoder’s numerical precision must therefore be interpreted with its scale. BSQ and the two-token representationBSQ 与双 token 表示

Independent scalar quantizers place a grid over each original feature. Vector quantization instead assigns an entire vector to a codeword. Kronos first learns a contextual transformation of the six features, then quantizes that latent vector. The encoder can place observations with related candle shape, activity and history in the same region even when their original coordinate-wise bins differ.逐特征标量量化，会在原始特征的各个坐标上分别划格子；向量量化则把整条向量分配给一个码字。Kronos 先对六维特征及其历史上下文学一个变换，再量化潜向量。这样，编码器可以把形态、成交活动与历史相关的观测放进同一区域，即使它们在原始坐标上属于不同格子。

The implementation projects six features into a hidden space, applies causal Transformer blocks, then projects each position into K coordinates; the released base tokenizer uses K = 20. Given an already normalized sequence, the causal mask prevents the encoder at t from reading later positions. Thus the code can depend on the preceding trend rather than just the current row. However, historical-window normalization can change earlier inputs when that window changes: a causal mask alone does not make the entire raw-data preprocessing causal at every earlier position.实现中，六维特征先投影到隐藏空间，经因果 Transformer，再把每个位置投影成 K 个坐标；已发布 base tokenizer 使用 K = 20。对于已经标准化的序列，因果掩码阻止 t 位置读取后续位置，因此编码可以利用此前走势。需要区分：更换历史窗口会改变归一化统计量，进而改变较早的输入；注意力的因果掩码不能单独保证整条原始数据预处理链在每个较早位置都严格因果。

A conventional learned vector codebook stores candidate vectors and assigns a latent vector to a nearby candidate. BSQ fixes the candidate geometry instead: its codewords are the 2ᴷ sign combinations with coordinates ±1/√K. The learned encoder adapts the data to these fixed regions. A normalization step places nonzero latent vectors on the unit sphere, and a sign test selects a vertex.常规可学习向量码本存储候选向量，再把潜向量分配给邻近候选。BSQ 则固定候选几何：码字由坐标 ±1/√K 的 2ᴷ 种符号组合构成，让可学习编码器把数据变换到适合这些区域的位置。归一化将非零潜向量放在单位球面上，符号测试负责选择顶点。

Why does taking signs select the nearest vertex? Let z be unit length and write a candidate as q = s/√K, where each sᵢ is ±1. All candidates have unit length, so minimizing squared Euclidean distance is equivalent to maximizing their dot product with z. Each coordinate can be chosen independently: match the sign of zᵢ.为什么取符号恰好能选中最近顶点？令 z 的长度为 1，把候选写成 q = s/√K，每个 sᵢ 只能取 ±1。所有候选长度都为 1，因此最小化欧氏距离平方，等价于最大化它与 z 的点积。每个坐标可以独立选择：与 zᵢ 同号即可。

A zero coordinate is a distance tie; the pinned Kronos code chooses −1. Its normalization also has an epsilon guard, so the unit-vector derivation assumes a nonzero input outside that guard. For z = (0.6, 0.8), the chosen vertex is (1/√2, 1/√2), with squared error approximately 0.0201. This is latent-space distortion; the learned decoder determines the error in OHLC and activity.坐标为零时，两种符号距离相同；本文固定版本的 Kronos 选择 −1。代码的归一化还有 epsilon 保护，上面的单位向量推导假定输入非零且未触及该保护。对 z = (0.6, 0.8)，选中的顶点为 (1/√2, 1/√2)，平方误差约为 0.0201。这是潜空间失真，OHLC 与成交活动的重建误差还取决于学到的解码器。

The encoder and decoder must learn how observations map to these fixed vertices. Sign partitioning discards latent magnitudes and within-region differences; the reconstruction objective trains the surrounding transformation. The result avoids a million-vector lookup table, but does not establish economic meaning for a token. A token ID is a learned code, not an ordinal price, a regime label or a trading instruction. Boundary sensitivity and downstream forecast performance need separate measurement.编码器与解码器负责学习观测到固定顶点的映射。符号划分舍弃潜向量幅度和区域内部差异，重建目标训练其前后的变换。这样可以避免百万向量查表，但不会自动赋予 token 经济含义。编号是学到的编码，不是有大小关系的价格、已识别的市场状态标签或交易指令。边界敏感性与下游预测表现需要分别测量。

BSQ solves the codebook problem, but a new bottleneck appears if the predictor still treats each full 20-bit code as one class: a 1,048,576-way output layer. The 20 bits describe logical state capacity. In the public implementation, the two IDs are torch.long tensors; embeddings and the neural network still use their configured numerical precision. This is quantization of market representations, and does not imply a packed 20-bit runtime tensor or an INT8/INT4 network.这里的 20 bit 描述逻辑状态容量。公开实现中的两个编号使用 torch.long 张量，embedding 与网络仍按各自配置的数值精度运行。被量化的是市场表示，不能据此推断运行时已打包成 20 bit，也不能推断网络本身采用 INT8／INT4。

Two pieces are a practical trade-off, not a mathematically unique optimum. They still need a useful division of information and a way to coordinate their predictions.节省来自共享和分解计算，不再为每个联合状态各自保留一套参数。保留组合数量，并不保证保留完全相同的有效建模能力。继续拆分可以缩小单个词表，却会增加有依赖的决策步骤及融合开销；若每个头只预测一位，就需要 20 次决策。两段是工程折中，不能称为数学上唯一最优。接下来还有两个问题：信息怎样分工，以及两个预测怎样协调。

Training loss versus financial objectives训练损失与金融目标

The coarse/fine interpretation is enforced by the objective. Reconstruction from only the full 20-bit code would allow information to be distributed arbitrarily between the two halves. Kronos also requires the first ten bits to reconstruct the input independently. Both branches recover all six features through separate input projections and a shared causal decoder. The first token is therefore trained to carry a lower-capacity reconstruction, rather than being assigned a market meaning in advance.粗细分工由训练目标施加。若只训练完整 20 bit 重建，信息可以任意分散到两半；Kronos 还要求前 10 bit 独立重建输入。两条路径经不同输入投影与共享因果解码器恢复全部六个特征。因此，第一个 token 被训练成容量较低的重建表示，而非预先定义好的经济状态。

The prefix loss makes the first half useful under a smaller information budget; the full-code loss lets the second half contribute additional detail. That creates an inductive bias aligned with generating the first half before the second. The second half is not explicitly trained as an additive numerical residual, and “coarse/fine” assigns neither price/volume nor daily/minute semantics. The chosen bit semantics remain learned.前缀重建损失迫使第一半在较小信息预算下也能工作，完整重建损失则允许第二半补充细节。这给表示施加了与“先生成第一半、再生成第二半”一致的归纳偏置。第二半没有被显式训练成可加的数值残差；粗细也不对应价格／成交量或日线／分钟线，具体 bit 含义仍由模型学习。

Seen as rate–distortion design, the first ten bits must support a usable reconstruction, while twenty bits provide a larger budget. Both losses encourage this progression in aggregate. They do not enforce that the full reconstruction has lower error for every individual bar, and the second group is not a separate residual quantizer.从编码率与失真的角度看，前 10 bit 就要支持可用重建，完整 20 bit 再提供更大预算。两项损失在总体上鼓励这种分工，但没有约束每根 K 线的完整重建误差都小于前缀重建，也没有把后半组实现成一个独立残差量化器。

This structure is justified by how the code will be predicted, not by a claim of better reconstruction. Hard signs introduce a training problem: their derivative is zero almost everywhere. A straight-through estimator keeps discrete forward values but substitutes an identity gradient through the sign step; this is a surrogate, not the exact derivative. Why coarse and fine tokens are conditional粗细 token 为什么要条件生成

The factorized output must preserve the joint distribution of the two codes. For a fixed history, suppose only (A, a) and (B, b) are possible, each with probability one half. Independent heads can estimate both marginals correctly yet assign one quarter to each of four pairs. Half the resulting probability then lies on combinations absent from the target distribution. This is a distributional error even before the decoded output is evaluated as a return signal.分解输出词表后，仍需保留两个编号的联合分布。固定一段历史，假设下一对只有 (A, a) 和 (B, b) 两种可能，各占一半。独立头能够估对两个边际分布，却会给四种配对各分配四分之一概率，使一半概率落到目标分布没有的组合上。这首先是分布估计误差，随后才会影响解码结果及收益信号。

Kronos uses the probability chain rule: select a coarse ID, then predict the fine ID conditioned on that selection and the history. To write the general model, let aₜ and bₜ denote the two IDs at time t, and Hₜ the visible history. The toy example’s A/B/a/b are candidate values, whereas aₜ and bₜ below are variables. The factorization is an exact identity; how well finite neural networks approximate its factors is an empirical question. Reversing the order is mathematically possible, while the prefix-reconstruction objective motivates this chosen order.Kronos 使用概率链式法则：先选择粗编号，再根据这个选择及历史预测细编号。写成一般模型时，令 aₜ、bₜ 表示 t 时刻的两个编号，Hₜ 表示可见历史。上面例子的 A／B／a／b 是候选值，这里的 aₜ、bₜ 则是变量。下面的分解是精确的概率恒等式，有限神经网络能把各项逼近多好，才是实验问题。数学上也可以反过来分解；前缀重建目标为当前顺序提供了结构上的理由。

At each observed position, two embedding lookups are concatenated and projected into one hidden vector. Calendar embeddings are added. A causal Transformer backbone uses RMSNorm, rotary position embeddings, attention and SwiGLU feed-forward layers. For Kronos-small, the predictor has 8 blocks, hidden width 512 and 8 attention heads. The first output head produces logits for aₜ₊₁.每个已观测位置先查两张 embedding 表，把结果拼接后投影成一个隐藏向量，再加上日历 embedding。因果 Transformer 主干使用 RMSNorm、旋转位置编码、注意力与 SwiGLU 前馈层。Kronos-small 的预测主干有 8 层，隐藏宽度为 512，注意力头数为 8。第一个输出头给出 aₜ₊₁ 的 logits。

The dependency layer uses the sampled coarse embedding as a query into historical hidden states. This lets the fine head retrieve context conditioned on the selected coarse state, while reusing one backbone pass. Concatenating a coarse embedding with the last hidden state would be a simpler alternative; this cross-attention layer is a particular implementation of conditioning, not the only one implied by the chain rule.条件依赖层把采样到的粗 embedding 当作 Query，查询历史隐藏状态，让细输出头按已经选择的粗状态提取上下文，同时复用一次主干计算。把粗 embedding 与最后的隐藏状态直接拼接，是更简单的备选实现；交叉注意力是实现条件化的一种选择，概率链式法则并不唯一要求这一层。

Training introduces another mismatch: at inference the fine head receives a model-chosen coarse ID, which may be wrong. Feeding only perfect coarse targets during training would leave that situation unseen. The current script therefore samples the coarse condition from detached logits; both cross-entropies still target the true next IDs. Earlier bars remain real encoded observations, shifted by one position for next-bar supervision.训练还要面对一种错配：推理时，细头收到的是模型选出的粗编号，可能有误；若训练时只给正确粗编号，这种情形就没有被见过。当前脚本因此从停止梯度的 logits 中采样粗条件，两个交叉熵仍以真实下一编号为目标。此前各根 K 线依旧使用真实观测的编码，通过错开一个位置完成下一根监督。

Path generation and sampling limits路径生成与采样边界

Autoregression specifies temporal dependence through a sequence of one-step conditional distributions. Each generated bar becomes context for later bars, so a sampled rise, decline or activity change can affect the rest of that path. This makes pathwise return and risk statistics available in principle. A shared generator and variable horizon are useful properties for research, but the implied dependence still requires validation against observed sequences.自回归用一系列下一步条件分布表示时间依赖。每根生成的 K 线进入后续上下文，使已经采样到的上涨、下跌或成交变化能够影响余下路径。研究上可据此计算逐路径的收益和风险统计量，并在不同预测长度下复用生成器；路径依赖是否符合实际序列，仍需验证。

The cost is serial generation and feedback from model errors into later context. A one-shot multi-horizon model can reduce sequential latency and can also be designed to model dependencies; it is not inherently incapable of joint prediction. Kronos chooses a reusable next-step generator. Its paper’s AR ablation does not establish that this choice beats every one-shot method, and the public wrapper has a separate final-decode horizon limit described below.代价是串行生成，以及误差进入后续上下文后继续传播。一次输出多步的模型可以减少串行延迟，也可以设计成保留联合依赖，不能断言它天然做不到联合预测。Kronos 选择的是可重复调用的下一步生成器；论文的自回归消融没有证明它优于所有一次性多步方案，公开接口还存在下文的最终解码长度限制。

A rollout begins by encoding the history. Each step runs the backbone on the current token window, samples one coarse ID, samples one fine ID conditioned on it, and appends the pair. When the window is full, it drops the oldest pair. The implementation recomputes the window each step; this path does not implement a KV cache.生成从编码历史开始。每一步都对当前 token 窗口运行主干，先采样一个粗编号，再根据它采样细编号，然后把这一对追加到末尾。窗口满了就丢掉最早的一对。当前路径每一步都会重新计算窗口，没有实现 KV cache。
