The listed company is New China Life Insurance, A-share 601336 / H-share 01336. NBV discounts the expected future after-tax shareholder benefits from newly sold life policies, after allowing for required capital costs. Premium is the customer’s payment; NBV is an actuarial estimate; reported profit recognizes earnings under accounting rules. These quantities describe different stages and must not be added together.研究对象是新华人寿保险股份有限公司，A 股 601336、H 股 01336。新业务价值（NBV）把新售寿险合同预计产生的未来税后股东利益折现，并考虑持有必要资本的成本。保费是客户交来的钱，NBV 是精算估计，净利润是按会计规则确认的收益。三者描述不同阶段，不能加在一起。

Embedded value combines adjusted net assets with the value of existing in-force business after capital costs. It excludes the value of policies not yet sold. The report itself warns that definitions and assumptions can differ across companies.内含价值由调整后净资产和扣除资本成本后的有效业务价值组成，不包含将来尚未售出的新保单。投资回报、赔付、费用、退保与折现率变化，都可能改变估计。因此，市值相对内含价值的倍数低，本身不足以证明便宜。年报也明确提醒，不同公司的定义与假设可能不同。

Value growth needs sound assumptions and sufficient capital价值增长仍需检验假设与资本需求

New China Life shows genuine improvement in disclosed insurance measures, but the evidence does not justify treating its earnings as a stable, low-risk compounding stream. Better persistency and insurance service results support the operating improvement; bancassurance supplied 77.0% of the 2025 NBV increment, while nine-month 2026 net profit fell 32.2% and capital ratios declined from the previous September.新华披露的保险经营指标确有改善，但还不能把它的收益当作平稳、低风险的复利来源。继续率与保险服务业绩改善，支持经营进步的判断；同时，2025 年 NBV 增量的 77.0% 来自银保渠道，2026 年前三季度净利润下降 32.2%，偿付能力比率也低于上年同月。

An owner should pay for new policies only to the extent that their future cash benefits survive realistic assumptions and capital costs. NBV is a discounted estimate of those benefits, not an additional pool of cash on top of profit. Its reported growth is a useful starting point, but a valuation should test how much value remains when investment returns are lower and acquisition costs or policy lapses are less favorable. The published 50-basis-point return sensitivity cuts 2025 NBV by 32.1%; this makes the assumption material to the purchase price without proving that the stressed outcome will occur.所有者愿意为新保单付多少钱，应取决于其未来现金利益能否经受现实假设与资本成本的检验。NBV 是这些利益的折现估计，不是在净利润之外又多出的一笔现金。披露的增长可以作为研究起点，但估值必须检查投资回报降低、获客费用或保单失效情况不利时，价值还能保留多少。官方将投资回报假设降低 50 个基点后，2025 年 NBV 减少 32.1%；这说明假设会实质影响合理买价，却不意味着压力情形必然发生。

The capital-allocation question is whether the next unit of growth earns enough for the capital it locks up. The stronger NBV and persistency data favor further investigation of the new-business franchise. Lower comprehensive solvency, however, prevents equating that growth with immediately distributable value. For shareholder analysis, new-policy value, capital retained to support it, and cash available after contractual obligations must be assessed together. Expanding premiums, paying dividends and increasing investment risk all compete for a finite balance-sheet buffer; choosing among them cannot be judged by premium growth alone.资本配置的关键，是下一份增长能否为被占用的资本赚到足够回报。NBV 与继续率改善，值得继续研究新单经营能力；综合偿付能力下降，又提醒我们不能把增长等同于立即可分配的价值。判断股东收益时，应把新单价值、支撑新单而留存的资本，以及履约之后剩余的现金放在一起。扩张保费、支付股息和增加投资风险，都在使用有限的资产负债表缓冲，不能只用保费增速评价这些选择。

The inverse case is a business whose sales success outruns its economic value. Greater reliance on bancassurance could become a weakness if access to customers requires higher commissions, more generous guarantees or policies that lapse sooner than assumed. If incentives reward new-premium volume more strongly than value after capital costs and later persistency, the apparent growth could arrive before its costs become visible. This article has not established such incentive arrangements or adverse behavior. It identifies what must be checked in channel disclosures, product terms and remuneration policies before declaring the growth durable.逆向情形是：销售成功跑在经济价值前面。如果扩大银保销售需要更高佣金、更重保证利益，或保单比假设更早失效，渠道贡献增加就可能成为弱点。如果激励更重视新单规模，而不是扣除资本成本后的价值与后续持续率，增长可能先被看见，成本却晚些显现。本文并未证实公司采用这样的激励或出现这些行为，而是明确：认定增长能够持续之前，需要核对渠道披露、产品条款与薪酬政策。

A holder can turn the thesis into three observable tests: whether channel value margins and persistency hold up, whether service earnings improve without demanding increasingly optimistic assumptions, and whether capital headroom can support the strategy. Improving profit driven mainly by market gains would not by itself strengthen the life-business thesis. Deteriorating persistency or value margins together with weaker capital would require rebuilding it, even if total premiums kept rising. For a new researcher, a favorable base-case EV multiple is insufficient: test the valuation under lower-return assumptions, keeping the in-force-value sensitivity separate from total EV.持有者可以把依据落到三项可观察的检验：渠道价值率与继续率能否维持，保险服务收益能否在不依赖更乐观假设的情况下改善，资本余量能否支撑经营安排。主要由行情推动的利润反弹，并不单独强化寿险经营判断；如果继续率或价值率与资本同时走弱，即使总保费仍增长，也应重建持有依据。准备研究买入的读者，则不能停留在基准 EV 倍数看似便宜：还要用较低回报假设复核估值，且始终区分有效业务价值与总 EV。

The actionable research conclusion is to require more evidence of durable channel economics and capital resilience before paying for the headline NBV growth. This is not a conclusion that the shares should be avoided at every price; no current price, required return or sufficiently complete downside valuation has been established here. The next step is to bound sustainable shareholder value and compare that range with the chosen A- or H-share price, using the scope checks in the five-insurer comparison.可以执行的研究结论是：在为醒目的 NBV 增长付出估值溢价之前，先取得渠道收益能够持续、资本能够承受波动的更多证据。这不等于认定它在任何价格都不值得买；本文尚未核定当前价格、所需回报率，也没有完成足够全面的不利情形估值。下一步应先界定可持续股东价值的范围，再与选定的 A 股或 H 股价格比较，并用五家险企对照中的口径检查避免误比。
