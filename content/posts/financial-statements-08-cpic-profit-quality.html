Why an insurer needs a different cash-flow reading保险公司的现金，为什么要换一种读法

A manufacturer usually receives cash after selling goods. An insurer receives premiums in exchange for obligations that may be settled years later. Investing that money is part of the business, but the balance is not entirely shareholder property. Claims, maturities, surrenders and capital requirements come before an unrestricted distribution. The earlier lesson’s CFO-minus-capex shortcut therefore cannot be carried over as insurance owner earnings.制造企业通常在交付商品后收款；保险公司收取保费，同时承担可能多年以后才履行的赔付与给付义务。运用这些资金投资是经营的一部分，但账上的钱并不全属于股东。赔款、满期给付、退保和资本要求，都约束着分配。因此，前面用“经营现金流减资本支出”估计现金的简式，不能直接套成保险公司的所有者收益。

Life insurance and P&C insurance earn money differently: life policies accumulate long-duration obligations, while P&C analysis focuses more directly on claims and expenses relative to earned insurance revenue. Read the group’s profit together with the operating subsidiaries. Underwriting improves while life new-business growth weakens承保改善，但寿险新业务增长承压

CPIC’s underwriting evidence is improving, but its latest life-business value growth has weakened. The 2026 nine-month combined ratio of 96.2% and operating-profit growth of 5.6% support a more resilient operating assessment than the 20.3% decline in reported profit alone suggests. The 6.5% fall in life NBV prevents a blanket conclusion that every business is improving.太保的承保经营有改善证据，但最新寿险价值增长有所走弱。2026 年前三季度财险综合成本率 96.2%、营运利润增长 5.6%，说明经营韧性比单看净利润下降 20.3% 得出的印象更强；寿险 NBV 下降 6.5%，则表明新业务增长仍有压力。

Valuation should reflect recurring distributable cash rather than a favorable year’s profit. The 2025 proposed dividend was 30.3% of operating profit, but that ratio does not establish surplus cash. The parent’s RMB 11.537 billion profit and the group’s RMB 53.505 billion profit belong to different accounts. A conservative valuation should start with sustainable insurance earnings, normalize investment returns over a range of conditions, then allow for additions to or releases of required capital and the ability of subsidiaries to send cash upstream. Using 2025 reported profit as an unchanged annual base would conceal the volatility visible in 2026.估值应以可持续分配的现金为依据，不能直接外推有利年份的利润。2025 年拟分红相当于营运利润的 30.3%，但这并不能证明剩下的都是闲置现金。母公司净利润 115.37 亿元与集团归母净利润 535.05 亿元，分属不同层面的账。保守估值应从可持续的保险收益出发，在不同市场条件下估计正常投资回报，再考虑必要资本的新增占用或释放，并核对子公司上划现金的能力。把 2025 年净利润原样当作今后的年度基数，会掩盖 2026 年已经显现的波动。

Capital allocation should reinforce the profitable underwriting business rather than force premium growth. A lower combined ratio is useful only if claims are adequately reserved and pricing remains sustainable. Life premiums rising while NBV falls is a reason to scrutinize product economics before celebrating sales. On the evidence examined here, retained capital earns no automatic presumption of good use: new policies and investments need to justify the capital they absorb, while dividends need to remain supportable after a less favorable investment year.资本配置应巩固有盈利能力的承保业务，而不是追求保费规模本身。综合成本率下降，只有在赔付准备充分、定价能够持续的前提下才有意义。寿险保费增加而 NBV 下降，要求先检查产品经济性，再评价销售增长。按本文已有证据，不能默认留存的资本一定用得好：新保单与投资都应解释所占资本能够获得什么回报，分红也应能承受投资年份转弱。

The inverse test is whether today’s attractive measures could conceal tomorrow’s cost. A better combined ratio could be less reassuring if it relied on reserve releases that later reverse; stable operating profit could be misleading if its assumed investment return became too optimistic. A sales system rewarded mainly for volume could also encourage business that consumes capital without creating enough value. These are mechanisms to test, not findings of misconduct: this case has not established such reserve behavior or remuneration arrangements. The relevant checks are claims development, the operating-profit reconciliation, and the actual sales and management incentive measures.逆向检验要问：今天好看的指标，会不会把成本留给明天？如果综合成本率改善依赖日后需要补回的准备金释放，含义就不同；如果营运利润采用的长期投资回报假设过于乐观，平稳也可能掩盖风险。若销售考核偏重规模，还可能鼓励消耗资本、价值不足的业务。这些是需要检验的机制，不是已经发现的违规事实：本文并未证实公司存在上述准备金行为或薪酬安排。应进一步核对赔款发展、营运利润调节过程，以及真实的销售和管理层考核指标。

For an existing holder, the decision rule is to recheck the business thesis, not react mechanically to the net-profit headline. At each comparable reporting period, record the P&C ratio and reserve development, life NBV and persistency, and subsidiary capital plus parent cash. If only investment marks weaken while those operating and capital indicators remain sound, the evidence primarily calls for a more conservative return estimate. If underwriting, policy value and the capacity to distribute cash deteriorate together, the operating thesis itself needs revision. A researcher should complete that reconciliation before converting an apparent earnings recovery into a higher valuation.对已有持仓的读者，可执行的规则是复核经营依据，而不是看到净利润标题就机械买卖。每逢同口径报告，记录财险综合成本率与赔款发展、寿险 NBV 与继续率，以及子公司资本和母公司现金。如果主要是投资估值转弱，而经营与资本仍稳健，首先需要下调的是过于乐观的回报估计；如果承保、保单价值和分配能力一起恶化，就应重估持有依据。研究者也应先完成这张核对表，再决定利润恢复是否值得更高的估值。

The present conclusion is therefore specific: CPIC merits further study for its insurance operations, while its purchase value remains conditional on a price that leaves room for investment volatility, life-business weakness and capital needs. No current A/H-share price or required return has been established here, so the evidence does not yet support a buy or add decision. The New China Life case examines the value assumptions more closely; the five-insurer comparison brings these distinct businesses together.因此，当前结论应落在具体位置：太保的保险经营值得继续研究，但买入价值取决于价格能否为投资波动、寿险走弱和资本需求留下余地。本文尚未核定当前 A/H 股价格及所需回报率，现有证据还不足以形成买入或加仓结论。下一期用新华保险进一步检验价值假设，再在五家险企对照中综合比较。
