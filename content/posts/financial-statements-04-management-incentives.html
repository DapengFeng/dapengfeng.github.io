A shop manager can earn a bonus for higher sales while the owner waits longer for cash. Both read the same accounts, but benefit from different outcomes. This is an agency problem: the person allocating the owner’s money may pursue a different objective. A useful assessment of management compares the incentive terms, the actions taken and the economic results.店长可能因为销售额增长拿到奖金，股东却要等更久才能收钱。两人看同一份账，但各自获益的条件不同。这是代理问题：替股东管理资金的人，可能追求另一套目标。评价管理层，需要把激励条款、实际行动和经济结果对照起来。

Incentives are a mechanism, not a judgment of character激励是一种机制，不是对人品的评判

Munger calls this a central rule of management. When reading accounts, apply it by identifying the outcomes that the compensation terms reward. A sales-only target can encourage longer payment terms for customers; a short-term return target can discourage worthwhile long-term investment. These are possible effects to test, not accusations about a specific company.芒格把这视为管理中的重要原则。用于读财报，就是查清薪酬条款究竟奖励哪些结果。只看销售额，可能鼓励延长客户账期；只看短期回报，可能抑制有价值的长期投入。这些都是有待检验的影响，不是对具体公司的指控。

A US company’s 10-K can incorporate governance information by reference to a proxy statement. Apple’s FY2025 10-K refers to its 2026 DEF 14A. Match the performance period, not just the year on the document cover. Otherwise you may evaluate the wrong incentive plan.美国公司的 10-K 可以通过引用代理声明补充治理资料。Apple 2025 财年 10-K 引用了 2026 年 DEF 14A。应核对的是考核期间，不能只按文件封面的年份配对，否则可能读错激励方案。

Case · Two measurement periods in Apple’s compensation plan真实案例：Apple 薪酬方案中的两种考核周期

These measures reward different outcomes. Revenue and operating income measure annual operating results. Relative total shareholder return (TSR, including reinvested dividends) compares market returns over several years. Neither directly measures sustainable owner earnings or the return on each new project. Keep that distinction in mind when interpreting the plan.这些指标奖励的并不是同一种结果。收入与营业利润衡量一年的经营成果；相对股东总回报 TSR（含股息再投资）比较多年的市场回报。两类指标都没有直接计量可持续的所有者盈余，也没有直接衡量每个新增项目的回报。解读方案时，需要分清这些区别。

The filing also caps the number of units that can vest at the target number when Apple’s absolute TSR is negative, even if its relative ranking is higher. This changes the incentive when that condition applies; it does not establish that capital allocation is sound. To assess outcomes, compare investment, net share dilution and cash distributions over the same period, then examine the prices paid and the future cash those decisions are expected to generate.文件还规定：若 Apple 的绝对 TSR 为负，即使相对排名更高，最终可获得的股票单位也不能超过目标数量。这改变了该条件下的激励，却不能证明资本配置合理。评价结果时，还须在同一期间核对投资支出、股份净稀释程度与现金分配，再检查为这些决策付出的代价，以及预期能获得的未来现金流。

Management must choose what to do with the surplus管理层要决定，剩下的钱用在哪里

After allowing for maintenance needs, working capital, taxes and necessary liquidity reserves, management still has choices to make. Retained accounting earnings are not automatically cash available to spend; start with the cash assessment in part 2. Then reconstruct several years of actual uses of funds from the cash-flow statement and notes. The following uses compete for funds; they have no fixed order of priority.满足维持经营、营运资本和纳税方面的资金需求，并保留必要的流动性后，管理层仍然面临选择。会计上的留存收益并不自动等于可以花的钱，应先按第二期的方法判断可用现金，再从现金流量表和附注还原多年的资金用途。以下用途需要在有限资金中作出取舍，没有固定的优先顺序。

  • Reinvest in the business Ask how much additional after-tax cash the project can generate, when that cash will arrive and how much further investment is needed. Existing high returns do not automatically extend to a new product or market.继续投入本业追问项目能带来多少新增税后现金、何时收到、还需追加多少投入。原有业务的高回报，不会自动延伸到新产品或新市场。
  • Repay debt or retain liquidity Consider interest saved, refinancing risk, debt covenants and the ability to survive a downturn. Cash required for seasonal operations is not idle surplus.还债或保留流动性考虑可节省的利息、再融资风险、债务契约限制及度过经营低谷的能力。满足季节性经营所需的现金，并不是闲置余额。
  • Pay dividends Returning surplus cash lets owners choose another investment. A distribution can be sensible when attractive internal projects are scarce; it also reduces company cash and does not create value out of nothing.分红把剩余资金交还股东，让他们另作选择。公司内部缺少回报有吸引力的项目时，这可以是合理做法；分红同时减少公司现金，并不是凭空增加价值。
  • Repurchase shares Examine the price paid, the net reduction in shares outstanding after stock compensation, and the financial flexibility that remains. Buying shares is itself an investment decision.回购股份看付出的价格、考虑股权激励新增股份后股数净减少了多少，以及回购后还剩多少财务余地。买回股票本身也是投资决策。
  • Acquire another business Count the cash paid, shares issued, debt assumed and resources needed for integration. Compare future cash benefits with the full cost; buying earnings is not the same as generating them through the existing business.收购其他企业把支付的现金、发行的股份、承担的债务和整合所需的资源一起算入代价，再与未来现金收益比较。收购带来的利润，与原有业务自身增长产生的利润不是同一回事。

A useful record of management’s decisions has two columns: what was promised when capital was committed, and what subsequent disclosures show. Profit growth accompanied by repeated expensive acquisitions may be less impressive than flat profit with substantial cash returned to shareholders. Compare total value received per share, financial resilience and future earning power—not the size of the company alone.记录管理层决策时，可以分成两列：投入资本时承诺了什么，后来的披露显示了什么。利润增长若伴随着持续高价收购，未必比利润持平、却向股东返还大量现金更好。应比较每股获得的总价值、财务韧性和未来盈利能力，而不只是公司规模。

Check what actually happened把奖励规则与实际用钱对照

Apple’s FY2025 cash-flow statement records repurchases of 90,711 and dividends and dividend equivalents of 15,421, in USD millions. These figures show disclosed uses of cash. They do not establish that the incentive plan caused those decisions or that the repurchase price was attractive. Read the cash-flow statement, the notes on share counts and the compensation terms together.Apple 2025 财年现金流量表记录回购支出 90,711、股利及股利等价款支出 15,421，单位为百万美元。这些数字反映了已披露的资金用途，不能单独证明激励制度导致了这些选择，也不能证明回购价格划算。把现金流量表、有关股数的附注和薪酬条款放在一起读。

Practice · Does a buyback automatically increase the annual cash bonus?练习：回购能自动提高这里的年度现金奖金吗？

Not through an increase in earnings per share (EPS) alone: the disclosed financial measures are sales and operating income, not EPS. The long-term award uses relative TSR, rather than a direct EPS target. Check the actual compensation terms instead of assuming all executive pay rewards the same metric.仅靠每股收益（EPS）上升不能提高这项奖金：这里披露的财务指标是收入与营业利润，不是 EPS。长期奖励使用相对 TSR，也不是直接考核 EPS。应核对实际薪酬条款，不能假设所有管理层薪酬都奖励同一个指标。

Summarize your findings in a short record: the metric, its weight, the measurement period, payout limits, economic costs left out, the actual decision and the subsequent result. A potential conflict of interest is a reason to investigate; attributing a decision to that conflict requires further evidence.把阅读结果整理成一份简表：指标、权重、考核期限、奖励限制、未计入的经济成本、实际决策及后续结果。潜在利益冲突提供了调查方向；要认定某项决策由这种冲突导致，还需要更多证据。
