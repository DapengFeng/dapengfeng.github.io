These measures reward different things. Revenue and operating income examine an annual operating result. Relative total shareholder return (TSR, including reinvested dividends) compares a market outcome over several years. Neither is a direct measurement of sustainable owner earnings or the return on each new project. A sound interpretation keeps that distinction visible.这些指标奖励的并不是同一件事。收入与营业利润关注一年经营结果；相对股东总回报 TSR（含股息再投资）比较多年市场结果。两者都没有直接计量可持续所有者盈余，也没有直接衡量每个新增项目的回报。解读时要保留这种区别。

The filing also caps vesting at the target number when Apple’s absolute TSR is negative, even if the relative ranking is stronger. This changes the incentive at the boundary; it does not certify good capital allocation. To assess outcomes, compare investment, net dilution and cash distributions across the same period, then examine the price and future cash expected from those decisions.文件还规定：若 Apple 的绝对 TSR 为负，即使相对排名更高，兑现数量也不能超过目标数量。这改变了边界上的激励，却不能为资本配置质量背书。评价结果时，还须在同一期间核对投入、净稀释与现金分配，再检查这些决策付出的价格和预期获得的现金。

Management must choose what to do with the surplus管理层要决定，剩下的钱用在哪里

After maintenance needs, working capital, tax and necessary liquidity are covered, management still has a choice. Retained accounting earnings are not automatically cash available to spend; start from the cash assessment in part 2. Then reconstruct several years of actual uses of funds from the cash-flow statement and notes. Treat the following as competing uses, not a fixed ranking.满足维持经营、营运资本、税款和必要流动性之后，管理层仍然面临选择。会计上的留存收益并不自动等于可以花的钱，应先接上第二期对现金的判断，再从现金流量表和附注还原多年资金用途。下面这些用途彼此竞争，没有固定优先顺序。

  • Reinvest in the business Ask what incremental after-tax cash the project can produce, when it arrives and how much additional investment is needed. Existing high returns do not automatically extend to a new product or country.继续投入本业追问项目能带来多少新增税后现金、何时收到、还需补投多少。原有业务的高回报，不会自动延伸到新产品或新市场。
  • Repay debt or keep liquidity Consider interest saved, refinancing risk, covenants and the ability to survive a downturn. Cash required for seasonal operations is not idle surplus.还债或保留流动性比较节省的利息、再融资风险、契约限制及穿越低谷的能力。满足季节性经营所需的现金，并不是闲置余额。
  • Pay dividends Returning surplus lets owners choose another investment. A distribution can be sensible when attractive internal projects are scarce; it also reduces company cash and is not free value added.分红把剩余资金交还股东，让他们另作选择。内部缺少划算项目时，这可以是合理做法；分红同时减少公司现金，并不是凭空增加价值。
  • Repurchase shares Examine the price paid, net share-count reduction after stock compensation, and remaining financial flexibility. Buying shares is itself an investment decision.回购股份看付出的价格、扣除股权激励等新增股数后的净减少，以及回购后财务余地。买回股票本身也是投资决策。
  • Acquire another business Count cash, shares issued, debt assumed and integration needs. Compare future cash benefits with the full commitment; buying earnings is not the same as generating them organically.收购其他企业把现金、发行股份、承担债务和整合需求一起算入代价，再比较未来现金收益。买来的利润，与原有业务自己长出的利润不是同一回事。

A useful management record has two columns: what was promised when capital was committed, and what subsequent disclosures show. Profit growth accompanied by repeated expensive acquisitions may be less impressive than flat profit with substantial cash returned. Compare total value received per share, financial resilience and future earning power—not the size of the company alone.一份有用的管理层记录只需两列：投入资本时承诺了什么，后来的披露证明了什么。利润增长若依赖持续高价收购，未必比利润持平、却不断向股东返还现金更好。应比较每股得到的总价值、财务韧性和未来盈利能力，而不只是公司规模。
