The owner’s next question is the price paid for the shares. A buyback can increase earnings per share mechanically while destroying value if too much is paid. Ernst & Young’s report, pp. 49–50, gives an unqualified opinion on the consolidated financial statements under US GAAP. The audit seeks reasonable assurance that the financial statements are free of material misstatement. It does not guarantee that every figure is exact, endorse the business strategy or certify the stock’s value. Management remains responsible for the statements.安永在第 49–50 页对按美国 GAAP 编制的合并财务报表出具无保留意见。审计的目标是获取财务报表不存在重大错报的合理保证。这不等于保证每个数字都绝对精确，也不代表认可公司的经营战略或股票估值。管理层仍对报表负责。

The critical audit matter concerns uncertain tax positions. That is an invitation to read Note 7 carefully: a significant estimate required difficult audit judgment.
