A transparent range with explicit assumptions is more useful than relabeling 98,767 as an exact owner-earnings figure.不能。至少需要估计维持竞争能力必须投入的支出，以及正常经营现金水平，包括异常税款、周转时点的影响。股份支付的经济影响和未来再投资同样重要。明确假设、给出范围，比把 98,767 改名为精确的所有者盈余更有用。

Finish with a one-page owner’s memo最后写一页股东研究笔记

In this case, the cash bridge is inside what we can verify. Sustainable maintenance spending, customer retention and a defensible valuation still require evidence. A memo should preserve that boundary instead of turning a completed spreadsheet into a completed investment decision.在本案例里，现金勾稽是已经能够核查的部分；持续维持投入、客户留存和有依据的估值仍需补充证据。研究笔记应保留这条边界，不能因为表算完了，就写成投资判断也完成了。

Use six short entries. Keep facts, hypotheses and open estimates distinct; place the evidence beside the conclusion. A responsible first reading can end with “further research,” without forcing a buy or sell answer.用六项短记录完成初读。把事实、假设和待估计项分开，证据紧挨结论。一份合格的初读可以落在“继续研究”，不必强行给出买卖答案。

01

Fact · Cash事实 · 现金

CFO of 111,482 less equipment spending of 12,715 gives 98,767. This calculation is verified; sustainable owner earnings are not yet estimated. Source: p. 33.经营现金 111,482 减固定资产支出 12,715，得到 98,767。算术已核对，可持续所有者盈余仍待估计。来源：第 33 页。

02

Hypothesis · Business quality假设 · 生意质量

Services growth invites a hypothesis about continued use of the device ecosystem. The disclosure does not by itself establish retention or pricing power; compare mix, customer behavior and distribution rules.服务增长引出设备生态持续使用的假设，披露本身尚不能证明留存或定价权；继续比较结构、客户行为和分发规则。

03

Fact and limit · Incentives事实与边界 · 激励

The 2026 proxy documents annual sales/operating-income incentives and multi-year relative TSR awards. Their existence does not establish why a particular capital decision was made.2026 年代理声明披露年度收入／营业利润奖励与多年相对 TSR 奖励。制度存在，尚不能证明某项资金决策的动机。

04

Failure test · Liquidity失败检验 · 流动性

Schedule principal, commercial paper and other obligations against usable liquidity. The annual totals do not establish the lowest cash balance during a stressed year.把本金、商业票据及其他义务与可用流动性按期对应。全年总额尚不能证明压力年份中的最低现金余额。

05

Open estimate · Value待估计 · 价值

Maintenance needs, normalized cash and incremental investment returns still need evidence. Without those estimates and a dated price, there is no completed purchase judgment.维持投入、正常现金与增量投资回报仍需证据。没有这些估计和对应日期的价格，就没有完成买入判断。

06

Revision rule · Next evidence重审条件 · 下一份证据

Revisit the thesis if customer economics weaken, capital needs rise, or distributions erode required liquidity. Specify the observation and its period before seeing the next result.客户经济性走弱、资本需求增加，或分配侵蚀必要流动性时，重审原判断。在下次结果出现前写清观察指标和期间。

Compensation source: Apple 2026 Proxy Statement, printed pp. 45–47; covering FY2025 awards.薪酬依据：Apple 2026 年代理声明第 45–47 页，覆盖 2025 财年奖励。

The method is reusable: describe the business, connect the statements, estimate owner cash, explain returns, examine incentives and liquidity, then compare value with price. For each conclusion, ask both what the numbers establish and what could make our explanation wrong.读下一家公司时仍可沿用：描述生意、连接报表、估计所有者现金、解释回报、检查激励与流动性，最后比较价值与价格。每项结论都同时回答：数字证明了什么，我们的解释又可能错在哪里。
