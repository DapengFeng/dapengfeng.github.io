Hanging upside down and hovering are different force balances倒挂与腾空，必须分开解释

On the underside of a horizontal surface, suction pulls toward that surface while gravity still points down. For stationary contact, suction must balance both weight and the surface’s normal reaction. In free air, there is no road to supply support. Hovering or holding altitude then requires upward force to balance weight; a ballistic airborne arc can instead have less thrust than weight, or none. Read this way, the Roadster raises a design question larger than its launch time: how much of a car’s available force can become something its controller actively shapes? Motor torque selects a demand within the tyre limit; suction changes that limit; jets contribute force beyond the tyre contact. Together they offer more ways to respond to the driver, but also more conditions under which the response can change.沿着这条线看，Roadster 提出的设计问题比起步成绩更大：一辆车可用的受力范围，有多少能够由控制系统主动塑造？电机转矩在轮胎限制内提出需求，负压改变这条限制，喷流在接触力之外增加贡献。三者让车辆拥有更多响应驾驶者的方式，也带来更多会改变响应能力的条件。

The strongest demonstration of this architecture would be a car that knows the force it can still deliver, allocates it between acceleration, steering and attitude control, and reduces its demands coherently as grip, gas or thermal margin falls. Peak force makes the manoeuvre possible. Predictable force, available at the right instant and repeatable under stated conditions, is what would make the combination a convincing vehicle.最能证明这套架构价值的，是车辆知道自己此刻还能给出多少力，能够协调加速、转向与姿态控制，并在附着、储气或温度余量下降时，有序地收回超出能力的需求。峰值受力让动作成为可能；在正确时刻提供可预期的响应，并在明确条件下再次实现，才会让这些装置真正组成一辆令人信服的车。

The first design decision is now clear: identify the active constraint before adding hardware. When grip limits the car, suction may help; when wheel power limits it, the fan competes for that power; a jet adds force while consuming stored gas.
