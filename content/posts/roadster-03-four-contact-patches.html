The total force hides the first limit总受力会掩盖先到极限的车轮

Keep the 2,000 kg car and 4,905 N of added downforce from Part 2. Now ask it to brake while turning. The total normal load remains 24.525 kN, yet one tyre in the example below carries 10.64 kN and another only 2.77 kN. A controller cannot spend those loads as if they belonged to one large tyre.沿用第二期的 2,000 kg 车辆与 4,905 N 额外下压力，现在让它一边制动、一边转弯。四轮法向载荷之和仍是 24.525 kN，但下面的算例中，一条轮胎承担 10.64 kN，另一条只有 2.77 kN。控制器无法把这些载荷当成属于同一条巨大轮胎的资源，任意支取。

The design task is to keep useful force available at each contact patch. That requires three checks: how the load moves, how tyre capacity changes with that load, and how much of the capacity is already being used to turn. Only then is it meaningful to decide where more downforce should act.设计要解决的是每个接触区还能提供多少有用的力。需要先检查三件事：载荷怎样转移，轮胎能力怎样随载荷改变，以及转弯已经用了多少余量。之后，才能判断新增下压力应该作用在哪里。

The geometry and tyre curve in this installment are explicit assumptions, not Tesla specifications or measured Roadster performance. The calculations are quasi-static force budgets: they omit body-motion transients, road irregularities, detailed slip behaviour and the mass and power cost of the downforce device already discussed in Part 2.本期的几何参数与轮胎曲线均为明确给定的假设，不是 Tesla 规格或 Roadster 实测性能。计算属于准静态受力预算：省略车身运动瞬态、路面起伏、详细滑移响应，以及第二期已经讨论的下压力装置质量与功耗代价。

First balance the four normal loads先把四轮载荷算平

Take a 2.8 m wheelbase L, equal 1.7 m tracks t and a centre-of-mass height h = 0.5 m, halfway between the axles. Use g = 9.81 m/s². Downforce D acts vertically on the vehicle centreline; a fraction β reaches the front axle and the rest the rear. For the load snapshots, β = 0.5. Positive longitudinal acceleration aₓ means driving forward; braking has a negative value.取轴距 L = 2.8 m、前后轮距均为 t = 1.7 m，质心位于两轴正中、离地 h = 0.5 m，重力加速度 g = 9.81 m/s²。下压力 D 沿车身中线竖直作用，其中 β 分配到前轴，其余分配到后轴；载荷快照取 β = 0.5。纵向加速度 aₓ 向前为正，制动时为负。

Normal load is the road reaction perpendicular to the surface at each tyre. Write the front and rear axle totals as Nf and Nr. Acceleration transfers this load from front to rear because the tyre forces act at road level while inertia acts through the higher centre of mass. The transferred amount Qₓ follows from pitch-moment balance. It is a redistribution: adding it at the rear subtracts the same amount at the front.法向载荷是路面对轮胎、垂直于路面的支撑力，前后轴的总量分别记作 Nf 与 Nr。加速时，轮胎作用力位于路面，惯性作用通过更高处的质心，俯仰力矩平衡要求载荷从前轴转移到后轴。转移量 Qₓ 是重新分配的载荷：后轴增加多少，前轴就减少多少。

During a turn, use the magnitude of lateral acceleration aᵧ. The amount transferred from the inner side to the outer side is Qᵧ. It is half the resulting outer-minus-inner load difference. Let λ = 0.60 be the front axle’s share of that transfer. It is an effective model parameter; real suspension geometry, roll stiffness and unsprung masses determine the split, as discussed in SAE 930763.转弯时取横向加速度 aᵧ 的大小。从内侧转移到外侧的载荷为 Qᵧ，外侧与内侧因此增加的载荷差是它的两倍。取 λ = 0.60，表示前轴承担这次横向载荷转移的 60%。这是模型中的等效参数；实际分配由悬架几何、侧倾刚度与非簧载质量等共同决定，参见 SAE 930763。

The plus sign belongs to the outer tyre and the minus sign to the inner tyre. At aₓ = −0.6g and aᵧ = 1g, 2.10 kN transfers to the front and 5.77 kN to the outside. Summing the four results still gives mg + D = 24.525 kN. A negative wheel load would mean the assumed four-contact state has ended; it cannot be repaired by simply clipping that value to zero.加号对应外侧轮，减号对应内侧轮。在 aₓ = −0.6g、aᵧ = 1g 时，2.10 kN 载荷转移到前轴，5.77 kN 转移到外侧；四轮相加仍为 mg + D = 24.525 kN。如果算出负载荷，就说明所假设的四轮接地状态已经失效，不能简单把负值截成零后继续沿用同一组平衡式。

Stiffer suspension can reduce body roll without eliminating this load transfer. In this model, the total lateral transfer is set by mass, acceleration, centre-of-mass height and track. Changing its front–rear distribution changes which axle pays the larger tyre-capacity penalty. Lowering the centre of mass addresses the total transfer itself.更硬的悬架可以减少车身侧倾，却不等于消除载荷转移。在这个模型中，横向转移总量由质量、加速度、质心高度与轮距决定；改变前后分配，则改变哪一轴承担更多轮胎能力损失。降低质心，才直接减少这里的转移总量。

More load buys less than proportional grip增加载荷，抓地力却不按比例增加

Part 2 used a constant friction coefficient. That makes the sum of four tyre capacities depend only on total normal load. A load-sensitive tyre breaks that simplification: increasing normal load raises peak force, but the effective coefficient can fall. Measured nonlinear tyre data in Kasprzak, Lewis and Milliken’s vehicle-optimisation study explicitly includes this dependence.第二期使用恒定附着系数，因此四条轮胎能力之和只取决于法向总载荷。轮胎的载荷敏感性会改变这个结果：法向载荷增大时，峰值力继续增加，但等效附着系数可能下降。Kasprzak、Lewis 与 Milliken 的整车优化研究使用的实测非线性轮胎数据，就包含这种依赖关系。

A tyre develops force through deformation and relative motion in its contact patch. Longitudinal slip describes a mismatch between wheel rolling speed and ground speed; slip angle describes the difference between where a wheel points and where it travels. The force builds with these motions and reaches a peak under particular conditions. The calculation below keeps only that peak capacity; it does not solve the slip needed to produce it.轮胎通过接触区的变形与相对运动建立力。纵向滑移描述车轮滚动速度与地面行进速度的差异；侧偏角描述车轮指向与实际行进方向的差异。受力随这些运动建立，并在特定条件下达到峰值。下面的计算只保留这个峰值能力，不求解产生它所需的滑移状态。

Use a deliberately simple peak-force law C(Fz), with reference load F₀ = 4,905 N, reference coefficient μ₀ = 1.5 and exponent p = 0.88. The exponent illustrates sublinear growth; it is not a fitted tyre measurement. Setting p = 1 recovers the constant-coefficient model. Temperature, pressure, camber, surface and slip conditions would require actual tyre data.这里用一个简化的峰值力函数 C(Fz)：参考载荷 F₀ = 4,905 N，参考系数 μ₀ = 1.5，指数 p = 0.88。这个指数用于呈现次线性增长，没有拟合某款轮胎的实测数据；p = 1 时就回到恒定附着系数模型。温度、胎压、外倾角、路面与滑移条件的影响，仍需真实轮胎数据确定。

Because this curve bends downward, starting from equal tyre loads and transferring some load to the outer tyre loses more capacity at the inner tyre than it gains at the outer one. The axle keeps the same total normal load, but its peak-force sum falls. That is why the outer tyre cannot fully compensate for the inner tyre’s unloading.这条曲线向下弯曲。从原本均匀的两轮向外侧转移载荷、加剧载荷不均时，内侧轮损失的能力大于外侧轮接收同样载荷所增加的能力。车轴的法向总载荷没有变，峰值力之和却下降了。这就是外侧轮无法完全补回内侧轮卸载损失的原因。

Even before transfer, added downforce has diminishing returns in this example. Increasing all four loads by 25% increases their summed capacity by , about 21.7%. The balanced-load sum rises from 29.43 to 35.82 kN; the braking-and-turning load snapshot gives 35.32 kN. Neither sum proves that the car can use all of it in a desired direction while also satisfying yaw balance.即使没有载荷转移，这个算例中的下压力也存在边际收益递减。四轮载荷都增加 25%，峰值力之和增加的是 ，约 21.7%。均匀载荷时，总能力从 29.43 增至 35.82 kN；前面的制动转弯载荷快照得到 35.32 kN。这两个总和都没有证明：在满足横摆力矩平衡的同时，车辆能够把全部能力用在所需方向上。

Choose a balance with room left over选择仍有余量的下压力分配

Keep D = 4,905 N and vary only its front fraction β. First isolate steady cornering: no longitudinal acceleration, a centred mass and no imposed yaw moment. Under the small-steering-angle approximation, neglecting tyre-to-body force-projection differences, each axle must supply half the required lateral force. Spare rear capacity cannot simply fill a front deficit while preserving this moment balance.保持 D = 4,905 N，只改变分给前轴的比例 β。先单独考察稳态转弯：没有纵向加速度、质心居中、没有额外横摆力矩。采用小转角近似，忽略轮胎坐标到车身坐标的受力投影差异，前后轴各承担一半横向力。后轴的剩余能力，不能在保持力矩平衡的同时直接填补前轴缺口。

For each candidate lateral acceleration, recompute the four loads and the two axle capacities. Both axles must meet demand, and all four loads must remain positive. Increasing acceleration raises demand while also increasing the transfer that reduces tyre capacity. Solving these coupled constraints gives a force-budget upper bound, not a measured cornering limit.对每个候选横向加速度，都重新计算四轮载荷和两轴能力。前后轴都要满足需求，四轮载荷也都要为正。加速度提高，一方面增加需求，另一方面又通过载荷转移削弱轮胎能力。联立这些约束，得到的是受力预算上界。

A front fraction of 30% gives about 1.58g; 50% gives 1.70g. The unconstrained peak is near 56% front and 1.74g, where the two axle bounds meet. Compared with 50:50, that is only about a 2.2% gain. At the peak, the inner-front load is just 254 N. Optimising this number alone leaves little room for unloading disturbances.前轴分到 30% 下压力时，上界约为 1.58g；分到 50% 时，约为 1.70g。不另设轮载余量的峰值出现在前轴约占 56%、加速度约为 1.74g 的位置，两轴上限在这里相遇。相对前后各半，仅提高约 2.2%，此时内侧前轮只剩 254 N 载荷。只追求这个峰值，几乎没有为卸载扰动留出空间。

Make the reserve explicit. As an illustrative screening rule, require every wheel to retain at least 500 N. This is a chosen comparison threshold, not a safety standard. At a specified target of 1.65g, accept only balances that satisfy both axle-force demands and that minimum load; the highlighted interval in Figure 3 is the resulting model-allowed range. For p = 0.88, it runs from about 53.3% to 70.1% front downforce. Changing the assumed tyre exponent changes that interval too. The p = 0.84, 0.88 and 0.92 cases are sensitivity scenarios, not a confidence interval from measurements. The useful conclusion is a shortlist for investigation. Lowering the centre of mass or changing transfer distribution can preserve similar force capacity with more wheel-load reserve in these assumptions. Measure those constraints before selecting hardware; 56% is not a universal target.这组计算用于筛选值得继续核查的方向。在当前假设下，降低质心或改变载荷转移分配，能够在保持相近受力能力时留下更多轮载余量。但降低质心可能受空间布置限制，改变 λ 也需要调整几何与刚度，其瞬态影响尚未计算。应先核查这些约束，再选择硬件；56% 并不是通用目标。

Finish the braking-and-turning budget把开头的弯中制动算完

One contact patch transmits both longitudinal and lateral force. With peak capacity C, a friction circle approximates the shared budget. Kapania’s Stanford thesis introduces this coupling. For example, a tyre with C = 8 kN already supplying 6 kN laterally has only about 5.29 kN left longitudinally.同一个接触区同时传递纵向力与横向力。峰值能力为 C 时，可以用摩擦圆近似它们共用的预算。Kapania 的斯坦福博士论文用这一关系引入两者的耦合。例如，C = 8 kN 的轮胎已经提供 6 kN 横向力时，纵向只剩约 5.29 kN。

Real boundaries depend on slip and can be asymmetric. Brach and Brach explain why a circle or ellipse is not a complete tyre-response model. Here it is a screening constraint: a force vector outside it fails the assumed budget; a vector inside it still needs compatible slip states and vehicle dynamics.真实边界依赖滑移状态，也可能不对称。Brach 与 Brach 的分析说明了摩擦圆或椭圆为何不能替代完整轮胎响应模型。这里把它作为筛选约束：落在外面的力向量不满足假设预算，落在里面的向量仍需要相容滑移状态与整车动力学支持。

One explicit allocation for all four tyres给四条轮胎规定同一套分配规则

Return to the original −0.6g longitudinal, 1.0g lateral snapshot, with β = 0.5, λ = 0.6 and D = 4,905 N. Continue using the small-steering-angle approximation in body coordinates. Let b be the positive braking magnitude in units of g. Fix the front brake share γ at 0.6 and split each axle’s braking equally left/right. The floor must keep its pressure distribution底板要守住的是压力分布

A fan-assisted floor creates a pressure difference over an area. The resulting downforce depends on the pressure integral; its front–rear balance depends on where that pressure acts. Equal total force does not imply equal pitch moment. McMurtry’s stationary inverted demonstration is evidence that fan-assisted downforce can exist without forward speed. It does not establish Roadster hardware or performance on an uneven road.风扇辅助底板在一定面积上建立压差。总下压力取决于压差积分，前后分配取决于压力作用的位置；总力相同，不代表俯仰力矩相同。McMurtry 的静止倒置演示说明风扇辅助下压力可以不依赖前进速度，但没有证明 Roadster 的硬件方案或起伏路面上的表现。

Braking pitches the body, cornering rolls it, and a bump changes the gap between the floor and road. A larger leak demands more airflow to maintain the same pressure; if the fan cannot supply it, downforce falls. Changes that are stronger at one end can shift β even before the total force loss looks severe. A rigid body with a perfectly sealed floor hides this failure mode.制动带来俯仰，转弯带来侧倾，路面凸起改变底板与路面的间隙。漏气增加后，维持同样压差需要更多流量；风扇供不上，便会损失下压力。如果一端变化更强，即使总力损失尚不明显，β 也可能已经偏移。刚性车身与完美密封的底板模型会掩盖这个问题。

The skirt is part of that tradeoff. McMurtry’s vehicle-skirt patent describes controlling seal contact to reduce leakage while managing road friction and drag. It is a design disclosure, not a verification of every road condition. The useful suspension target is enough control of ride height, pitch and roll to preserve the pressure field, while retaining compliance for tyre contact and avoiding bottoming.裙边也参与这项取舍。McMurtry 的车辆裙边专利描述了通过控制密封接触来减少泄漏，同时管理与路面的摩擦和阻力。这是设计披露，不是对各种路况的验证。悬架需要充分控制离地间隙、俯仰与侧倾以保持压力分布，同时保留轮胎跟随路面的顺从性，并避免触底。

Simply making every spring and anti-roll bar stiffer does not solve both jobs. It changes body motion and load-transfer distribution, and can make rough-road contact harder to maintain. A usable specification pairs the allowed ride-height and attitude range with the downforce and balance that the floor actually sustains throughout that range.把弹簧和防倾杆全部加硬，无法自动同时完成这两项任务：它会改变车身运动与载荷转移分配，也可能使起伏路面的接地更难维持。可用的设计指标需要把允许的离地间隙与姿态范围，同底板在整个范围内能够保持的下压力及其分配一起给出。

The useful output is a set of conditional choices. Compare changes at equal downforce and include wheel-load reserve. Close the requested braking-and-turning motion with four force vectors and a moment balance. Then identify where pressure loss or redistribution breaks that same budget.
