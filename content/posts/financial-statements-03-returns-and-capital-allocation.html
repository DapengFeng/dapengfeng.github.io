What did the owner have to put in?赚到这些利润，股东先投入了什么？

Two businesses each earn 15 a year. One needs 100 of capital to operate; the other needs 300. If the earnings are equally durable and measured consistently, the first uses scarce capital much more efficiently. The income statement alone cannot reveal that difference. We need to connect each business’s earnings with the resources supporting them.两家企业每年都赚 15，一家只需投入 100 的资本，另一家却需要 300。若利润同样可持续、计算口径一致，前一家使用资本的效率就高得多。单看利润表，看不出这个区别；必须把利润与支撑利润的资源联系起来。

Buffett's 1979 shareholder letter makes this distinction concrete: Berkshire's earnings per share increased while its return on the larger capital base declined. He emphasized returns earned without excessive leverage or accounting distortions. The practical reading habit is to ask what generated an improvement before celebrating the percentage.巴菲特在 1979 年股东信中具体说明了这种区别：伯克希尔每股收益提高了，但相对于扩大的资本基础，回报率反而下降。他强调的是不依赖过度杠杆或会计扭曲的资本回报。落实到读财报，就是先追问业绩改善来自哪里，再判断回报率的意义。

This installment uses separate, invented mature businesses, rather than treating the first-year results from part 1 as those of a mature business. All monetary amounts in the fictional examples use the same arbitrary unit. The examples concern ordinary nonfinancial businesses. Banks and insurers require different treatment of financing, regulatory capital and liabilities; their deposits and insurance float cannot simply be inserted into the formulas below as ordinary industrial debt.本期另用几组虚构的成熟企业算例，不把第一期初创企业的首年数据直接当作成熟期的数据。虚构算例中的所有金额都使用同一种任意货币单位。讨论范围是普通非金融企业；银行和保险公司的融资、监管资本与负债需要另行分析，不能直接把存款、保险浮存金当作普通工业企业借款代入下列公式。

A high return on capital prompts a further question about the business. Buffett’s calculations measure the return; his emphasis on lasting business quality, which he credits to Munger, leads him to ask what sustains it. The See’s case below connects the two approaches.较高的资本回报率会引出一个经营问题：这样的回报为什么能够持续？巴菲特的计算衡量回报的高低；而他归功于芒格的思路，则重视企业能否长期保持竞争优势，追问回报靠什么维持。下面的喜诗糖果案例把两种思路联系起来。

ROE: distinguish operating strength from financingROE：分清经营能力与融资方式

Return on equity, or ROE, relates the profit attributable to common shareholders to their average book equity over the period. The denominator is an accounting amount, not the stock-market value of the company. Use the average because a year's profit is earned over time; a single year-end balance may be unrepresentative. Large midyear transactions may require a time-weighted average rather than a simple opening-and-closing mean.净资产收益率 ROE把归属于普通股股东的利润与该期间普通股股东的平均账面权益相比较。分母是会计数字，不是公司股票的市值。使用平均值，是因为全年利润是在一段时间内赚取的，年末某一天的余额未必有代表性；年中发生重大交易时，可能需要按时间加权，不能只取期初与期末的简单平均。

Here is profit attributable to common shareholders after interest, tax and any preferred dividends; is their average book equity. To see the effect of financing, compare two businesses with the same 100 of operating capital and the same 20 of annual earnings before interest and tax (EBIT). B finances half its capital with debt at 8% interest. Both pay 25% tax. Assume the financing balances remain constant and profits are distributed, so those balances also equal the annual averages.这里， 是扣除利息、所得税及优先股股利后归属于普通股股东的利润， 是其平均账面权益。现在比较两家企业：经营资本同为 100，年度息税前利润 EBIT 同为 20。B 用利率为 8% 的借款筹措一半资本，两家的税率都是 25%。假设融资余额保持不变、利润全部分配出去，因此表中资本余额也等于全年平均值。

Repurchases can also reduce book equity and lift ROE without improving operations. Asset impairments can shrink the denominator for later years; spending on internally developed brands and research may be expensed rather than recorded as assets. These accounting effects make cross-company rankings hazardous. When equity is tiny or negative, stop using ROE to rank businesses and examine the business and financing directly.回购也会减少账面权益，可能在经营没有改善时抬高 ROE。资产减值会缩小后续年份的分母；内部品牌建设与研发支出又可能已经计入费用，没有作为资产留在报表上。这些会计差异使跨公司的简单排名很容易失真。权益很小甚至为负时，就不要继续用 ROE 给企业排名，应直接检查经营与融资。

Try it: what if EBIT falls from 20 to 4?练习：如果 EBIT 从 20 跌到 4 呢？

Holding the example's capital and interest terms constant, A earns 4 × 75% = 3, so ROE is 3%. B’s EBIT of 4 is entirely offset by interest expense, leaving zero profit and zero ROE. Its operating business was no stronger than A’s when its ROE was 24%. Read debt maturities and liquidity alongside profitability: the profit calculation alone says nothing about whether principal can be repaid.保持算例中的资本和利息条件不变，A 的净利润为 4 × 75% = 3，ROE 为 3%；B 的息税前利润 4 全部被利息费用抵消，净利润和 ROE 都变成 0。之前 24% 的 ROE，也不代表 B 的经营能力比 A 更强。还要结合盈利情况，查看债务到期安排与流动性：这张利润计算表，并没有回答本金能否偿还。

ROIC: put operating profit over operating capitalROIC：让经营利润对应经营资本

To compare operating performance independently of the mix of debt and equity financing, use return on invested capital, or ROIC. Its numerator is after-tax operating profit before financing costs, often called NOPAT. Its denominator is the average capital committed to those same operations. ROIC is an analytical measure, not one standardized line that every annual report defines identically.想比较企业不受借款与股权融资比例影响的经营能力，可以看投入资本回报率 ROIC。分子是已扣除税款、未扣除融资成本的经营利润，常称 NOPAT；分母是支持这些经营活动的平均投入资本。ROIC 是分析指标，并不是每份年报定义都完全一致的标准报表项目。

In this simplified calculation, is a normalized tax rate on operating profit. It may differ from the reported effective tax rate when one-off items affect that rate. and are opening and closing invested capital. Do not put after-interest net income in this numerator while leaving debt-funded capital in the denominator: that mixes two different questions.这个简化计算里的 是经营利润适用的正常化税率，不一定等于受一次性事项影响的报表实际税率； 与 分别是期初、期末投入资本。不要把扣过利息的净利润放在分子，分母却仍包含由债务融资形成的资本；这样会混淆股东回报与全部经营资本回报。

Here invested capital means operating assets less non-interest-bearing operating liabilities such as trade payables. Required operating cash belongs in operating assets. Excess cash and unrelated investments are excluded from operating assets, and their income is excluded from operating profit. Borrowing is financing capital, not a trade payable to subtract here. This example has no leases, acquisitions or minority interests requiring further adjustments.本例把投入资本定义为经营资产减去应付货款等无息经营负债。维持经营所必需的现金算经营资产，超额现金与无关投资不算，其收益也不进入经营利润。有息借款是资本来源，不能在这里像应付货款一样扣掉。为便于入门，本例没有租赁、收购或少数股东权益需要额外调整。

For a real company, write down your adjustments. If leases are treated as financing capital, adjust the profit treatment consistently. When judging an acquisition, include the price actually committed, including goodwill; removing goodwill and then calling the resulting ratio management's acquisition return erases part of what shareholders paid. An asset-light business may have little recorded capital because earlier spending was expensed. A large ratio needs an economic explanation, not automatic praise.分析真实公司时，要把调整过程写出来。若把租赁视为融资资本，利润的调整口径也要与之保持一致。判断收购是否成功，应考虑实际投入的收购价，包括商誉；先删掉商誉，再把高比率称作管理层的收购回报，相当于抹去了股东付过的一部分钱。轻资产企业也可能因为历史支出已费用化而只有很少的账面资本。回报率很高时，需要解释背后的经营原因，不能直接认定企业经营出色。

Look beyond past average returns when judging the next investment判断下一笔投资，不能只看过去的平均回报

A strong existing business may have few attractive expansion opportunities. Suppose a mature operation earns 30 of annual NOPAT on 200 of capital. Management adds 60, and, once the expansion is fully operating, annual NOPAT reaches 42. The added capital appears to produce 12 a year: an incremental return of 20%. If the same investment lifts profit only to 33, the incremental return is 5%, despite the attractive original business.原有生意很好，也可能已经没有多少划算的扩张机会。假设一家成熟企业用 200 的资本，每年获得 30 的 NOPAT。管理层再投入 60，扩张项目全面投入运营后，年度 NOPAT 达到 42，新增资本看起来每年带来 12，增量回报为 20%。如果同样投入 60，却只把利润提高到 33，增量回报就只有 5%，尽管原有生意仍然不错。

Dividing the change in profit by the change in capital provides a research clue, not proof that the added capital caused the increase in profit. Selling prices for existing products may have risen, raw-material costs may have fallen, and a new factory may take years to reach capacity. Compare several years and explain which investments produced which earnings; use project cash flows when available. Growth creates value only if the future return compensates for the time and risk of committing the capital. Buffett discusses the importance of incremental returns in his 1992 letter.将利润增量除以资本增量，只能提供研究线索，不能证明利润增长就是新增资本带来的。原有产品可能涨价，原材料可能降价，新工厂也可能几年后才能达产。要比较多个年份，解释哪些投入对应哪些利润；有项目现金流资料时，就用它进一步分析项目回报。增长是否创造价值，取决于未来回报能否补偿资本投入的时间与风险。巴菲特在 1992 年股东信里讨论了增量回报的重要性。

Try it: profit rose by 3. Did the expansion succeed?练习：利润多了 3，扩张就成功了吗？

Not enough information. In the simplified steady example, 3 ÷ 60 = 5%. If we assume an appropriate required return of 10% on that capital, the investment needs to generate 6 of annual after-tax operating profit to meet the requirement. Earning 3 falls short by 3. The 10% is a teaching assumption, not a universal hurdle or Buffett's prescribed rate; a real decision also requires consideration of the investment’s timing, useful life, risk and remaining value.还不能这样下结论。按这个简化的稳定经营算例，3 ÷ 60 = 5%。若假设这笔资本适用的必要回报率是 10%，每年就需要 6 的税后经营利润才能达到要求，赚 3 还差 3。这里的 10% 只是教学假设，既不是统一门槛，也不是巴菲特规定的回报率；真实决策还要考虑投入时间、使用寿命、风险与剩余价值。

A moat needs an explanation that holds up against the numbers护城河，需要经得住数字检验的解释

If a business earns unusually high returns, why do competitors not take them away? That is the useful question behind a moat. A brand, a lower production cost or high costs for customers switching suppliers could provide an answer. The financial statements show outcomes; they do not prove the cause or guarantee that protection will persist.一家企业回报率很高，竞争对手为什么没有把利润抢走？这是“护城河”背后真正有用的问题。品牌、更低的生产成本、顾客更换供应商时面临的高成本，都可能提供解释。财报展示的是结果，并不直接证明成因，更不保证这种保护会一直存在。

Read segment disclosures, management's price/volume explanations and competitor reports together. A high gross margin is not a moat score: some strong businesses deliberately operate with low margins and rapid capital turnover. Write a falsifiable explanation, such as “customers renew despite a price increase,” and identify the disclosure that would show it failing.把分部披露、管理层对价格与销量的解释、竞争对手年报放在一起读。毛利率高不等于护城河深：有些优秀生意主动维持低毛利，通过资本快速周转赚钱。写下一个可以被证伪的解释，例如“提价后客户仍然续约”，并指出什么披露会说明这个判断失效。

Case · See’s grows earnings with little additional capital真实案例：喜诗如何用较少新增资本支撑利润增长

Looking back in 2014 on the 1972 See’s acquisition, Buffett reports about $4 million of annual pre-tax earnings on $8 million of net tangible assets. Dividing earnings by assets gives roughly 50%, a pre-tax return based on that historical asset definition. That ratio describes capital productivity, not the return earned by a buyer paying a different price.巴菲特在 2014 年回顾收购喜诗时，列出 1972 年约 400 万美元的年度税前利润和 800 万美元的净有形资产。用利润除以资产，得到约 50% 的税前回报率。这个比率按当时的资产口径衡量资本效率，并不等于按不同价格买入企业的投资者所获回报。

The mechanism behind that high ratio matters more than the ratio alone. Buffett’s 2007 letter, printed p. 7, explains that cash sales avoided receivables and a short production and distribution cycle limited inventory. Lasting customer preference supported pricing power. Together, these features allowed earnings to grow without requiring a proportionate increase in operating capital.比这个高比率本身更值得研究的，是形成它的机制。巴菲特在 2007 年股东信第 7 页解释：现款销售减少了应收账款，较短的生产与配送周期限制了存货占用，顾客长期的购买偏好支撑了定价能力。几项条件共同作用，使利润增长不必伴随经营资本同比例增加。

In the 2007 letter, “Businesses — The Great, the Good and the Gruesome”, Buffett connects See's competitive position with its limited need for additional capital. He reports required operating capital of $8 million at the 1972 purchase and $40 million in 2007: only $32 million more. That suggests a useful question when analyzing another company: how much extra capital did its growth absorb? These are historical, largely pretax comparisons, not a current valuation or a standardized ROIC series.在 2007 年股东信“优秀、良好与糟糕的生意”一节中，巴菲特把喜诗糖果的竞争地位与较少的新增资本需求联系起来。他列出的经营所需资本，从 1972 年收购时的 800 万美元增至 2007 年的 4,000 万美元，只增加了 3,200 万美元。读其他公司时，可以带着这个问题：它的增长究竟需要追加多少资本？这些是历史性的、主要涉及税前利润的比较，并非当前估值，也不是标准化的 ROIC 时间序列。

When analyzing another company, turn “strong brand” into a testable explanation. Do customers accept a price rise without disproportionate loss of volume or retention? Does the explanation still hold after comparing competitors and accounting for changes in the product mix? Read segment results, price/volume disclosures and working-capital notes across several years. If retention data is not disclosed, record the gap rather than inventing an answer.读下一家公司时，要把“品牌好”改写成能检验的解释：提价后，销量或客户留存是否出现不成比例的损失？与同行比较，并考虑产品结构变化后，这个解释还成立吗？对照多个年份的分部业绩、价格与销量披露，以及营运资本附注。留存数据没有披露，就把它列为缺口，不能自己补出答案。

Low-cost operators can have strong economics with modest margins. Conversely, a small denominator based on book capital can reflect expensed research or past write-downs as well as efficiency. Check what the ratio omits. A high historical return also does not prove that every new store, acquisition or product can earn the same return; an expansion must attract customers on terms that sustain the business’s returns.低成本经营者可以靠不高的利润率获得良好回报。反过来，账面资本很小，也可能受到研发费用化或过去减值的影响，不全是效率高。要检查比率漏掉了什么。历史回报高，也不代表每一家新店、每一次收购、每一种新产品都能获得同样的回报；扩张还得吸引顾客，并让交易条件足以维持企业的回报。

Practice · A 50% historical ratio: what is still missing?练习：历史比率约 50%，还缺什么？

Evidence that customer preference and cash conversion endure; returns on the next investment; and a separate valuation at the offered purchase price. A historical ratio does not answer all three.还需要证据判断顾客偏好与利润转化为现金的能力能否持续，估计新增投资的回报，并单独评估给定买价是否合理。一个历史比率不能回答这三件事。
