Follow the cash to the listed parent追踪现金是否到了上市母公司

A controlled bank’s assets and earnings enter the consolidated accounts, but its cash remains inside a regulated legal entity. The bank must meet depositor obligations and capital requirements before paying dividends. The same distinction applies to insurance subsidiaries and policyholder liabilities. The parent’s free-cash reconciliation is therefore more informative for distributions than group operating cash flow less equipment purchases.控股银行的资产和利润进入合并报表，但现金仍属于受监管的独立法人。银行先要满足存款支付与资本要求，才能分红；保险子公司也要先履行保单责任。分析股东分配时，母公司自由现金变动表比“集团经营现金流减购建设备支出”更有解释力。

The subsidiaries paid the parent 53,022. After the year’s 46,537 shareholder payment, 6,485 remained before subsidiary investment and other movements. The bank’s cash dividend was 5,752, far below its 24,711 attributable operating-profit contribution: recognition of earnings and receipt of cash are different events. This is a timing and capital-allocation distinction, not proof that the remainder was lost.子公司向母公司分红 53,022，减去年内实际付给股东的 46,537，尚余 6,485，之后还要考虑对子公司投资等用途。其中银行实际汇入的股息为 5,752，明显少于其 24,711 的归母营运利润贡献：确认利润与收到现金是两件事。这反映了分配时点和资本配置的差别，不能据此认定其余利润已经损失。

The 46,537 paid during calendar 2025 is also different from the 48,891 dividend attributed to FY2025, which includes a final payment proposed after year-end. Use the former to reconcile cash movements and the latter to discuss the fiscal-year payout policy; mixing them creates a false cash gap.2025 日历年实际支付的 46,537，与归属于 2025 年度、包含年后拟派末期股息的 48,891，也不是同一个期间口径。核对现金变动使用前者，讨论年度分配政策使用后者；混用会算出并不存在的现金差额。

The owner question is whether additional capital retained in each business earns an acceptable risk-adjusted return. Munger’s counter-test is to ask what would break the payout story: weaker upstream dividends, repeated capital injections, persistent investment shortfalls or cash distributions increasingly financed by borrowing. A convertible-bond inflow is financing, not evidence of improved underwriting.从所有者角度，要问留在各业务中的新增资本，能否取得与风险相称的回报。反过来检验，则要观察什么会破坏稳定分红的判断：子公司上划减少、持续注资、投资收益长期不足，或者分配越来越依赖融资。可转债流入是融资来源，不能证明承保能力改善。

Uneven business recovery; dividends depend on upstream cash集团改善不均，分红取决于现金上划

Ping An’s improvement is real but uneven. The evidence supports a recovering financial group, not uniform double-digit growth across its businesses. P&C underwriting improved and asset-management losses narrowed, while banking and the 2026 life NBV figures require separate scrutiny.平安的改善真实存在，但分布并不均匀。现有证据支持“多项业务修复中的金融集团”，尚不足以认定各业务都已进入稳定的两位数增长。产险承保改善、资产管理减亏值得肯定，银行表现与 2026 年寿险新业务价值则需要单独检验。

Owner earnings start with cash that can leave the subsidiaries. In RMB millions, the parent received 53,022 in subsidiary dividends and paid shareholders 46,537 during 2025. That leaves 6,485 before subsidiary investment and other movements; it is not all available for an additional payout. A defensible distribution estimate starts with sustainable upstream dividends, then allows for necessary parent expenses, financing obligations and capital injections. The group’s 134,415 operating profit is useful evidence about business performance, but it is not the starting cash balance for that calculation.所有者收益，要从子公司能够汇出的现金算起。以下金额均为人民币百万元。2025 年母公司收到子公司分红 53,022，向股东实际支付 46,537，两者相减剩下 6,485，尚未考虑对子公司投资与其他用途，不能全部视为额外可分配资金。估计未来分配能力，应从可持续的子公司分红出发，再考虑必要的母公司费用、融资义务和注资需求。集团营运利润 134,415 有助于判断经营表现，却不能直接充当这项计算的现金余额。

Capital allocation determines whether retained earnings help the owner. The bank’s smaller cash transfer than its attributable operating-profit contribution does not by itself indicate a loss: retained capital may support future earnings. It earns that justification only if incremental returns compensate for the risk and later improve distributable capacity. Likewise, asset-management loss reduction explains 64.6% of the group’s 2025 operating-profit increase. Treat the reduction as progress, but do not compound that one-year contribution indefinitely or assume further injections will earn adequate returns without evidence.留下来的利润是否有价值，要看资本配置的结果。银行汇入的现金少于其归母营运利润贡献，本身不说明资金损失；留存资本可能支撑未来盈利。但只有新增回报足以补偿风险，并最终改善可分配能力，留存才得到经济上的支持。同样，资产管理减亏贡献了 2025 年集团营运利润增量的 64.6%。可以肯定修复进展，却不能把这一年的贡献率一直外推，也不能在缺少证据时认定后续注资一定划算。

Inversion makes the distribution thesis falsifiable. A claim of improving owner returns weakens if upstream cash falls while recurring capital injections rise, or if distributions increasingly depend on new borrowing. Those conditions would matter even with higher adjusted profit. Incentives belong in this test: if remuneration rewards premium volume or adjusted earnings without charging for the capital and risk they consume, growth can benefit the reported metric before it benefits the owner. That is a risk to check against disclosed remuneration and capital-allocation outcomes, not a finding about Ping An’s actual incentive arrangements.逆向检验，要写出什么情况会推翻分配能力改善的判断。如果子公司上划现金下降、反复注资增加，或者分红越来越依赖新增借款，即使调整后利润仍增长，也应下调对股东回报质量的评价。激励机制也要纳入检验：如果考核奖励保费规模或调整后利润，却不充分计入资本消耗和风险，增长可能先改善考核指标，未必改善股东收益。这是需要对照薪酬披露和资本配置结果核查的风险，不是本文对平安实际考核机制已经作出的认定。

Use peers to test the explanation at the same business level. Compare Ping An Life with China Life and New China Life on new-business economics, persistency and capital strain under compatible actuarial assumptions; compare Ping An P&C with CPIC P&C and PICC P&C on underwriting and reserve development; compare the bank with banks. A strong subsidiary cannot erase a weak one merely because their profits consolidate into the same total. This business-level comparison is the appropriate basis for judging the group’s diversification.横向检验必须落在同一业务层面。平安寿险应与中国人寿、新华保险在可比精算假设下比较新单经济性、持续率与资本消耗；平安产险应与太保产险、人保财险比较承保和准备金发展；银行则与银行比较。优势业务不能因为合并报表汇总，就自动抵销弱项业务的经济问题。按业务逐项比较，才能判断集团的多元化究竟创造了多少价值。

Actionable conclusion: A holder can test a distribution-based thesis by updating three reconciled records at each reporting date: subsidiary earnings and cash transfers, capital retained or injected, and parent cash after distributions and financing. Keep the thesis only while the evidence supports sustainable transfers without progressively weaker capital coverage. For a new valuation, use a range of sustainable parent distributions and explicit capital needs rather than extrapolating the 10.3% operating-profit growth rate. The disclosures justify continued research into cash generation; they do not yet establish that the shares offer an adequate return at any particular price.可执行的结论：若持有逻辑是持续分红，每次披露后就更新三组能勾稽的记录：子公司利润与现金上划、留存资本与新增注资、分红及融资后的母公司现金。只有现金上划具有持续性、资本覆盖又没有不断削弱，这个持有逻辑才继续成立。进行估值时，应使用可持续母公司分配的区间，并明确资本需求，不能直接外推 10.3% 的营运利润增速。现有披露足以支持继续研究现金创造能力，但尚未证明股票在某个价格上具有足够回报。
