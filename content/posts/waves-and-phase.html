A is the amplitude, k the wave number, ω the angular frequency, and φ the initial phase. Wavelength λ = 2π/k, period T = 2π/ω, and phase speed v = ω/k. Freeze time to see a sinusoidal shape across space; fix a location to see an oscillation over time.

A 是振幅，k 是波数，ω 是角频率，φ 是初相位。波长 λ = 2π/k，周期 T = 2π/ω，相速度 v = ω/k。固定时间看空间，是一条正弦曲线；固定位置看时间，则是一个振动。

The vertical displacement does not mean that particles move along the propagation direction. In a transverse wave on a string, particles oscillate vertically as the wave travels horizontally.

图中的纵向位移不是质点沿传播方向的运动。对于绳上的横波，绳上质点上下振动，而波形沿水平方向传播。

Phase: one rhythm, different starting points

相位：同一个节奏，不同的起点

A phase difference tells us how far two equal-frequency waves are offset within a cycle. At zero, crests meet crests. At π, crests meet troughs.

相位差描述两列同频波在一个周期里错开了多少。相差 0 时，波峰对波峰；相差 π 时，波峰对波谷。

Where the model stops

模型的边界

This experiment illustrates ideal linear superposition. It does not model damping, dispersion, reflection, or nonlinear media. The wave-number control changes the spatial period while temporal angular frequency remains fixed, so it also changes phase speed. This toy model is not the dispersion relation of a particular fixed medium.

本实验演示理想线性叠加，未模拟阻尼、色散、反射或介质的非线性。调节波数改变空间周期；演示中的时间角频率固定，因此同时改变了相速度。这个玩具模型不能直接当作某一种固定介质的色散关系。
