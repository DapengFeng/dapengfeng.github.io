Use a small fictional business to isolate the effect of timing; these figures are not forecasts for Apple. The business starts with unrestricted cash of 10. Contracted receipts of 8 are expected before a specified payment date, when required payments of 12 and a further 5 of principal fall due. With no refinancing or asset sales, cash remaining after those payments would be 1. If receipts totaling 4 arrive after that date, the calculation for the payment date gives −3: a funding gap of 3.用一家虚构企业单独考察收付时点的影响；这些数字并非对 Apple 的预测。期初可用现金为 10，约定在某个付款日之前收到货款 8，当天必须支付的款项为 12，另有本金 5 到期。不再融资、不出售资产，付清当天到期款项后会剩下现金 1。若其中金额为 4 的回款延至这一天之后，付款日的测算结果就变成 −3，即出现 3 的资金缺口。

The same sales revenue can remain recognized in both scenarios. What changes is whether the cash arrives before a contractual payment is due. From the owner’s perspective, the concern is preserving the ability to keep operating; thinking in reverse tests the conditions under which that ability could be lost. Possible responses include retaining more liquidity, changing payment terms or securing a funding commitment in advance, rather than assuming a loan will always be available.两个情景下，同一批销售收入都可能已经确认。变化的是现金能否赶在合同约定的付款日之前到账。从股东的角度看，需要保住持续经营的能力；反向思考则检验什么情况下会失去这种能力。可考虑的应对方式包括多留一些流动性、调整付款条件或提前取得融资承诺，而不能假定到时总能借到钱。
