Premium cash arrives before the final cost is known保费先到账，最终成本后确定

That timing gap leaves money available for investment before claims are paid. Buffett calls it float. It still supports obligations to others. If ultimate claims and operating expenses exceed the premiums earned from the risk, underwriting losses are an economic cost of obtaining that funding. Investment returns must first cover that cost before the arrangement benefits shareholders.这个时间差，使保险公司在支付赔款前可以运用一笔资金，巴菲特称之为浮存金。但这笔钱仍然对应对他人的义务。如果最终赔款和经营费用超过承担这些风险获得的保费，承保亏损就是取得这笔资金的经济成本。投资收益首先要弥补这项成本，股东才可能从中受益。

Read the combined ratio as a cost decomposition把综合成本率拆开，才能解释改善

PICC’s combined ratio measures its disclosed underwriting costs relative to insurance service revenue. Its definition incorporates claims and expenses, reinsurance effects, insurance-finance items and the specified premium-reserve charge. It is not simply cash claims divided by premiums collected. The total is reported as a claims ratio plus an expense ratio. Below 100% indicates an underwriting surplus under that reported definition.人保披露的综合成本率，衡量相关承保成本相对于保险服务收入的比例；其定义包含赔款及费用、再保险影响、承保财务项目和规定的保费准备金计提，并非“已付赔款除以收到的保费”。公司将其拆成综合赔付率与综合费用率。低于 100%，表示按这套披露口径承保端有盈余。

From 2024 to 2025, the expense ratio fell 2.2 percentage points, while the claims ratio rose 1.3 points. The net effect was a 0.9-point improvement in the combined ratio. The accurate conclusion is that expense-ratio improvement more than offset claims-ratio deterioration. “Claims improved” would reverse the evidence. The next question is how much expense improvement reflects durable distribution and operating efficiency rather than a favorable mix that could change.从 2024 到 2025 年，费用率下降 2.2 个百分点，赔付率却上升 1.3 个百分点，合计使综合成本率改善 0.9 个百分点。准确结论是：费用率改善超过了赔付率恶化的影响。“赔付改善推动利润增长”反而与数据相反。接着应追查费用率的下降有多少来自持久的渠道和运营效率，有多少来自可能变化的业务结构。

The profit effect is large because the starting margin is thin. Published underwriting profit rose from 70.85 to 124.43, an increase of 75.6%, while service revenue grew 5.4%. Multiplying 2025 revenue by the rounded 2.4% margin gives about 122.78, not the reported 124.43; the rounded ratio cannot reproduce the precise profit. Use the disclosed profit for exact calculations.因为原有利润空间很薄，成本率小幅变化会造成利润大幅波动。披露的承保利润从 70.85 增至 124.43 亿元，增长 75.6%，同期保险服务收入仅增长 5.4%。用 2025 年收入乘以四舍五入后的 2.4% 利润率，只能得到约 122.78 亿元，无法精确还原 124.43 亿元；精确计算应采用披露的利润数。

Underwriting improves; durability needs a full-cycle test承保改善，持续性仍需跨周期检验

PICC’s disclosed underwriting performance improved, but durable low-cost float remains unproven. In 2025, lower expenses more than offset a higher claims ratio; in the first nine months of 2026, P&C underwriting profit rose 11.3% while group attributable profit fell 32.0%. The investment-related decline in group earnings does not overturn that underwriting evidence. It does show that better underwriting alone cannot stabilize group earnings.人保披露的承保表现确实改善，但低成本浮存金能否长期持续，仍未得到证明。2025 年费用率下降超过了赔付率上升的影响；2026 年前三季度财险承保利润增长 11.3%，集团归母利润却下降 32.0%。投资端影响下的集团利润回落，没有推翻承保改善的证据，也说明仅靠承保改善不足以稳定集团盈利。

Funding costs need to be assessed over a full underwriting cycle. The 2025 P&C underwriting profit of RMB 12.443 billion supports a favorable assessment of that year’s funding economics: under a definition that treats underwriting losses as the cost of float, an underwriting profit makes the cost negative. It does not establish a permanent advantage. Nor does a 97.6% combined ratio imply a −2.4% financing rate: the ratio uses insurance revenue, whereas a float-cost rate needs a consistently defined average float balance. Before assigning value to cheap funding, test ultimate claims and catastrophe losses across years, then estimate investment returns consistent with the liabilities. The cash will eventually have to meet policyholder claims.资金成本需要放在完整的承保周期内评估。2025 年财险承保利润 124.43 亿元，为当年的资金取得成本提供了积极证据：若把承保亏损作为浮存金成本，承保盈利就意味着这项成本为负。但这还不是永久优势。综合成本率 97.6% 也不等于融资利率为 −2.4%：前者以保险服务收入为分母，浮存金成本率则需要口径一致的平均浮存金余额。估计低成本资金的价值前，应跨年度检验最终赔款与灾害损失，再估计与负债相匹配的投资回报；这笔现金最终仍须履行对保户的承诺。

Owner earnings require disciplined capital allocation as well as underwriting profit. In 2025, minimum required capital grew faster than actual group capital; the life subsidiary’s core solvency ratio fell from 185% to 134.0%. Neither fact alone establishes distress, but both rule out treating group profit as cash freely available to shareholders. A conservative owner-earnings estimate must reconcile taxes, minority interests, required capital retention, subsidiary distributions and parent obligations. Additional capital should earn an adequate return for its risk. If a line cannot be repriced to achieve that return, reducing exposure is more valuable to owners than expanding premiums; this is a capital-allocation criterion, not a claim that PICC has already taken that action.所有者收益既依赖承保利润，也依赖资本配置纪律。2025 年集团最低资本的增长快于实际资本，寿险子公司核心偿付能力充足率从 185% 降至 134.0%。这两点本身不构成财务困境的证据，但足以要求我们逐项核对，而不能把集团利润直接视为股东可取走的现金。保守估计所有者收益，需要核对税费、少数股东权益、必要资本留存、子公司分红与母公司付款义务。新增资本必须取得与风险相称的回报；如果某类业务无法通过重新定价达到这一要求，收缩敞口比继续扩张保费更有利于所有者。这是资本配置的判断标准，不是对人保已经采取相关措施的断言。

Low-cost float can become expensive. In 2025, non-motor lines together lost RMB 1.815 billion, so motor profitability did not demonstrate sound economics across every line. An incentive system dominated by premium volume or current-year profit could encourage underpricing or optimistic reserve estimates; the evidence presented here does not establish that PICC uses such incentives. The favorable thesis would weaken if subsequent claims required repeated material reserve strengthening, expense savings disappeared after adjusting for business mix, or loss-making lines kept expanding without credible repricing. These observations would bear directly on underwriting discipline; a single catastrophe year or a decline in market values would need its own explanation.低成本浮存金也可能变成高成本负债。2025 年车险以外的险种合计亏损 18.15 亿元，车险盈利不能证明所有险种都具有良好经济性。若考核主要奖励保费规模或当期利润，就可能诱导低价承保或过于乐观的准备金估计；本文证据尚不能认定人保存在这种激励安排。如果后续赔款反复要求大额补提准备金、调整业务结构后费用改善消失，或者亏损险种持续扩张却缺少可信的调价措施，就应削弱对承保纪律的积极判断。这些信号直接关系到承保质量；单个灾害年份或市场价格下跌，则需要分别解释。

The next decision is to test durability before assigning a valuation premium. Extend the analysis with claims-development disclosures and several years of catastrophe-adjusted underwriting results, then reconcile the cash subsidiaries can distribute while retaining necessary capital. If underwriting remains profitable through that test and investment risks are adequately reflected, estimate conservative per-share distributable earnings and compare them with the dated price of the specific A or H share. If reserve or capital evidence fails the test, reduce the value assigned to low-cost funding.
