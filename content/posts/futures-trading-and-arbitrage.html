A trade can have a positive payoff at delivery and still run out of cash before delivery. Suppose a firm buys inventory and sells the matching futures: the price difference yields CNY 600 at delivery, and the assumed complete costs are CNY 300. Why might its futures account later need another CNY 540?一笔到期能赚钱的交易，也可能在到期前缺钱。假设一家企业买入一批现货，同时卖出数量匹配的期货：按到期交割计算，价差收入为600元，假设全部成本为300元。为什么它的期货账户后来还可能需要补入540元？

The answer connects contract rules, daily settlement and arbitrage pricing. Follow the cash before judging the model. This article covers exchange-traded futures in mainland China; all numerical examples are constructed, with rules checked on 6 November 2026. They are neither current quotes nor a historical strategy backtest.答案藏在合约规则、每日结算与套利定价的连接处。理解模型之前，先追踪现金。本文以中国内地交易所期货为范围，规则核对日期为2026年11月6日。所有数值例子均为构造算例，不是实时行情，也不是历史策略回测。

Start with what one contract represents先弄清一手合约代表什么

A futures contract fixes the underlying, quantity, quotation unit, delivery month and settlement terms. A long position gains when its futures price rises; a short position gains when it falls. Opening creates a position; closing offsets an existing position. Selling to open and selling to close are different instructions.期货合约规定标的、数量、报价单位、交割月份和结算条款。建立多头后，期货价格上涨带来盈利；建立空头后，价格下跌带来盈利。开仓建立持仓，平仓了结已有持仓。“卖出开仓”和“卖出平仓”是不同的指令。

Quantity and tick数量与最小变动价位
Read the multiplier and quotation unit together. A tick is the smallest permitted price change; tick value equals tick size times the multiplier.合约乘数要和报价单位一起看。最小变动价位是一档报价的大小；每手一档的盈亏，等于最小变动价位乘以合约乘数。
Contract month合约月份
Different months are separate instruments. A continuous or “main contract” chart is a data series, not an orderable contract that rolls itself.不同月份是不同合约。“主连”或连续行情是一条数据序列，不能把它当成会自动换月的可交易合约。
Margin and access保证金与交易权限
Margin reserves collateral rather than paying the full purchase price. Investor eligibility, position limits and the broker’s margin schedule must be checked for the specific product.保证金用于履约担保，不是付清标的价款。能否交易、持仓限额和期货公司的保证金标准，都要按具体品种确认。
Session and fees交易时段与手续费
Same-day closing is generally possible. Night sessions and the definition of a trading day vary; opening, closing and closing today’s positions can carry different fees.通常可以当天开仓、当天平仓。夜盘安排与交易日归属要按品种核对，开仓与平仓的手续费可能不同，平掉当日新仓与平掉此前持仓的手续费也可能不同。
Expiry and settlement到期与交割
Contracts end in physical delivery or cash settlement. The trading lot and the delivery unit can differ. Personal accounts may have to close before the last trading day.合约到期采用实物交割或现金交割。交易单位与交割单位可能不同，个人账户也可能需要在最后交易日之前平仓。

The SHFE settlement rules define daily settlement and minimum client margin; the risk-control rules cover limits and liquidation. The delivery rules restrict natural-person delivery positions. Product-specific provisions and current notices take precedence over a generic timetable. A price limit constrains permitted quotations; it does not guarantee an exit fill.上期所结算办法规定每日结算和客户保证金下限，风险控制办法规定限仓与强平等机制，交割办法限制自然人的交割持仓。具体品种条款与最新通知优先于概括性的时间表。涨跌停板限制可申报的价格，并不保证平仓委托能成交。

For a concrete distinction, the SHFE rebar specifications set a trading lot of 10 tonnes but a delivery unit of 300 tonnes. Trading one lot does not make one lot independently deliverable. The exchange’s trading guide explains same-day opening and closing; its older numerical schedules should not replace current product notices.一个具体区别是：上期所螺纹钢业务细则规定交易单位为10吨/手，交割单位却是300吨。可以交易一手，不代表这一手能够单独交割。交易所的合规交易指南解释了当日开平仓机制；其中较早的数值标准不能代替当前品种通知。

For the following arithmetic, assume an illustrative contract quoted in CNY per tonne, with 10 tonnes per lot. Its price is 2,000 and its margin rate is 10%. These assumptions do not specify a tradable product or its delivery unit.下面先假设一份用于讲解的合约：按元/吨报价，每手10吨，价格为2,000，保证金比例为10%。这些假设不对应某个实际品种，也没有规定它的交割单位。

P is the quoted price, M the quantity per lot, N the number of lots, V the notional value, m the margin rate and G the required margin. A 1% adverse price move loses CNY 200: 10% of the initial margin. Account return depends on all capital allocated, not just the minimum collateral.P是报价，M是每手数量，N是手数，V是名义价值，m是保证金比例，G是所需保证金。价格向不利方向变化1%，一手亏损200元，相当于初始保证金的10%。账户收益率的分母应考虑分配给交易的全部资金，不能只看最低保证金。

Return to the matched delivery case and assume a 60-day holding period. To make every financing charge visible, suppose the firm has arranged a CNY 25,000 loan for all 60 days at 3.65% annual simple interest: CNY 20,000 buys inventory and CNY 5,000 funds the futures account and liquidity reserve. Idle cash earns nothing. These are assumed financing terms, not a lending quote. Keep tax treatment consistent and assume no additional net tax expense in this example.回到能够匹配交割的情形，假设持有60天。为了把资金成本算全，设企业已安排一笔25,000元、占用完整60天的借款，按年利率3.65%计单利：20,000元买货，5,000元用于期货账户和备用现金，闲置资金不计利息。这是演算用的融资条件，不是贷款报价；各项价格采用一致的税务处理，并假设本例没有额外的净税费支出。

The budget is CNY 30/t, so the break-even futures sale price is 2,000+30=2,030. Selling at 2,060 leaves CNY 30/t, or CNY 300 for 10 tonnes. This is a break-even threshold for this funded trade, not a universal fair price. Before execution, use an obtainable cash purchase price and futures sale price, include the applicable tax cash flows, and replace every assumed charge with a verified quote or a stress estimate.每吨成本为30元，因此期货卖价达到2,000＋30＝2,030，才刚好保本。按2,060卖出，剩下每吨30元，10吨共300元。这是本例这笔交易的保本线，不是所有交易者共同的合理价格。执行前，要使用能够买到现货、卖出期货的价格，核对实际税款收付，再把表里的假设费用逐项替换为已确认的报价或压力估计。

For a storable asset, a simplified carrying-cost benchmark is F*=+financing+storage and other carry−income received while holding it, with all amounts valued consistently at maturity. Stock-index futures subtract dividends; commodity inventory may also provide a convenience benefit from having goods available. Borrowing or shorting physical inventory is often constrained, so a low futures price does not automatically permit the reverse trade. The CME fair-value calculation explains the financing/dividend relationship; Damodaran’s futures-arbitrage notes examine replication and its practical limits.对可储存资产，可以先用简化的持有成本基准理解定价：F*＝现货价＋融资与仓储等持有成本−持有期间收益，各项金额要换算到一致的到期时点。股指期货需要减去股息；商品库存还可能因为随时可用而带来便利收益。实物借入或卖空往往受到限制，因此期货价格偏低时，也不一定能反向复制套利。CME公允价值算例解释融资与股息的关系，Damodaran的期货套利讲义讨论复制条件及实际限制。

Reconcile the capital and the final cash把本金、补款和最后收到的钱对上

Now inspect the cash path. Start the futures account with CNY 3,060: CNY 2,060 of margin and CNY 1,000 available. In a constructed price path, futures rise to 2,200. Keep the margin rate at 10% and exclude fees from this account snapshot. The short loses CNY 1,400; equity falls to 1,660 while required margin rises to 2,200. The shortfall is CNY 540.现在检查资金过程。期货账户初始存入3,060元，其中2,060元被保证金占用，可用资金为1,000元。在一条构造路径中，期货升至2,200。保证金比例仍为10%，这张账户快照暂不扣手续费。空头累计亏损1,400元，账户权益降至1,660元，所需保证金却升至2,200元，缺口为540元。

If spot rises by the same CNY 140 per tonne, inventory gains CNY 1,400 on paper. That gain offsets the futures loss economically but cannot automatically pay the margin call. Selling the inventory early changes the original delivery strategy; borrowing more can change C. A workable arbitrage plan therefore needs both a terminal profit calculation and a funded liquidity path.如果现货也上涨140元/吨，库存账面增值1,400元。它在经济上抵消了期货亏损，却不能自动用来补保证金。提前卖掉库存会改变原来的交割方案，临时借款也可能改变成本C。因此，一套可执行的套利方案，必须同时说明最终利润和全过程的资金来源。

Allocate the CNY 25,000 at entry: CNY 20,000 pays for goods, CNY 3,060 enters the futures account, and CNY 1,940 stays outside as a cash reserve. When the CNY 540 shortfall occurs, transfer it from that reserve. The reserve falls to CNY 1,400; no new principal is borrowed. This transfer is not a second loss or a fee—the futures loss has already reduced account equity.开仓时，把25,000元分为三部分：20,000元付货款，3,060元转入期货账户，1,940元留在账户外备用。出现540元缺口时，从备用金转入540元，备用金剩下1,400元，并未新增借款。这次转账不是又亏了一次，也不是手续费；期货亏损已经反映在账户权益中。

Now suppose delivery occurs at 2,100 after 60 days. The goods bring in CNY 21,000; the short futures position has lost CNY 400 in total. Once settlement is complete and collateral is released, the futures account returns 3,060+540−400=CNY 3,200. For this cash reconciliation, assume interest and all other costs are paid at the end.再假设60天后按2,100交割。货物收到21,000元，期货空头从开仓到交割累计亏400元。结算完成、保证金解除占用后，期货账户可收回3,060＋540−400＝3,200元。为了核对最终现金，本例将利息和其他费用都安排在最后支付。

A personal futures account with no physical inventory has no cash-sale leg to offset its futures P&L. It must close within the permitted holding period and cannot use the delivery-profit identity to claim a locked return. Buying to close a short removes that existing position; it is not the same order as buying to open a new long.如果个人只是交易期货，没有持有现货，就不存在现货卖出所得这一腿，不能套用前面的交割利润等式，声称收益已经锁定。持仓应在允许的期限内了结。“买入平仓”是把已有空头平掉，和另建多头的“买入开仓”不同。

The reverse cash-and-carry trade requires borrowing and selling the physical asset, investing usable sale proceeds, and buying futures to obtain the asset for return at maturity. Borrowing fees, collateral restrictions and recall or delivery terms determine whether it is feasible. A convenience benefit from holding inventory is usually non-cash: it can explain pricing, but cannot simply be deducted as money received in the cash budget.反向期现交易需要先借入现货并卖出，运用可自由使用的卖出所得，同时买入期货，到期取得货物后归还。借货费用、抵押限制、提前召回与交割条款，都会影响可行性。持有库存带来的便利收益通常属于非现金利益：它可以解释定价，却不能直接当成现金收入，从资金预算中扣掉。

Calendar spreads: specify which difference you trade跨期套利：先把价差方向写对

Different delivery months respond to different inventory and supply conditions. A near-term shortage may support the nearby contract while expected new supply restrains a later one. A calendar spread trades this relative movement. Unlike the matched delivery identity above, two contracts expiring on different dates have no obligation to finish at the same price. CME’s spread overview distinguishes calendar, intercommodity and processing spreads.不同交割月份面对不同的库存和供给条件。近期缺货可能支撑近月，未来增产预期则可能压制远月。跨期组合交易的是这种相对变化。与前面的交割等式不同，两个到期日不同的合约，没有最终价格必须相等的约束。CME价差交易介绍区分了跨期、跨品种与产业链组合。

Use an independent example quoted in CNY/tonne: nearby 4,000 and deferred 4,100. Define D=deferred−nearby. Buy 10 tonnes of nearby exposure and sell 10 tonnes of deferred exposure, then close both at the same later time. Nearby closes at 4,050 and deferred at 4,110.另取一个独立算例，报价单位仍为元/吨：近月4,000，远月4,100。约定D＝远月−近月。买入10吨近月敞口、卖出10吨远月敞口，之后在同一时点平掉两腿，平仓价格分别为近月4,050、远月4,110。

Buying nearby and selling deferred benefits when D narrows; reversing the position benefits when D widens. Both prices can rise while the first combination makes money. If they rise by the same amount, gross spread profit is zero and trading costs still apply.买近卖远在D缩小时获利，反向组合在D扩大时获利。两个价格都上涨，前一种组合仍可能赚钱；如果两者上涨幅度相同，价差毛利为零，交易成本仍要支付。

Costs完整成本
