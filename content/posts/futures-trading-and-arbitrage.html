A trade can have a positive payoff at delivery and still run out of cash before delivery. Suppose a firm buys inventory and sells the matching futures: the price difference yields CNY 600 at delivery, and the assumed complete costs are CNY 300. Why might its futures account later need another CNY 540?一笔到期能赚钱的交易，也可能在到期前缺钱。假设一家企业买入一批现货，同时卖出数量匹配的期货：按到期交割计算，价差收入为600元，假设全部成本为300元。为什么它的期货账户后来还可能需要补入540元？

The answer connects contract rules, daily settlement and arbitrage pricing. Follow the cash before judging the model. This article covers exchange-traded futures in mainland China; all numerical examples are constructed, with rules checked on 6 November 2026. They are neither current quotes nor a historical strategy backtest.答案藏在合约规则、每日结算与套利定价的连接处。理解模型之前，先追踪现金。本文以中国内地交易所期货为范围，规则核对日期为2026年11月6日。所有数值例子均为构造算例，不是实时行情，也不是历史策略回测。

Start with what one contract represents先弄清一手合约代表什么

A futures contract fixes the underlying, quantity, quotation unit, delivery month and settlement terms. A long position gains when its futures price rises; a short position gains when it falls. Opening creates a position; closing offsets an existing position. Selling to open and selling to close are different instructions.期货合约规定标的、数量、报价单位、交割月份和结算条款。建立多头后，期货价格上涨带来盈利；建立空头后，价格下跌带来盈利。开仓建立持仓，平仓了结已有持仓。“卖出开仓”和“卖出平仓”是不同的指令。

Quantity and tick数量与最小变动价位
Read the multiplier and quotation unit together. A tick is the smallest permitted price change; tick value equals tick size times the multiplier.合约乘数要和报价单位一起看。最小变动价位是一档报价的大小；每手一档的盈亏，等于最小变动价位乘以合约乘数。
Contract month合约月份
Different months are separate instruments. A continuous or “main contract” chart is a data series, not an orderable contract that rolls itself.不同月份是不同合约。“主连”或连续行情是一条数据序列，不能把它当成会自动换月的可交易合约。
Margin and access保证金与交易权限
Margin reserves collateral rather than paying the full purchase price. Investor eligibility, position limits and the broker’s margin schedule must be checked for the specific product.保证金用于履约担保，不是付清标的价款。能否交易、持仓限额和期货公司的保证金标准，都要按具体品种确认。
Session and fees交易时段与手续费
Same-day closing is generally possible. Night sessions and the definition of a trading day vary; opening, closing and closing today’s positions can carry different fees.通常可以当天开仓、当天平仓。夜盘安排与交易日归属要按品种核对，开仓与平仓的手续费可能不同，平掉当日新仓与平掉此前持仓的手续费也可能不同。
Expiry and settlement到期与交割
Contracts end in physical delivery or cash settlement. The trading lot and the delivery unit can differ. Personal accounts may have to close before the last trading day.合约到期采用实物交割或现金交割。交易单位与交割单位可能不同，个人账户也可能需要在最后交易日之前平仓。

The SHFE settlement rules define daily settlement and minimum client margin; the risk-control rules cover limits and liquidation. The delivery rules restrict natural-person delivery positions. Product-specific provisions and current notices take precedence over a generic timetable. A price limit constrains permitted quotations; it does not guarantee an exit fill.上期所结算办法规定每日结算和客户保证金下限，风险控制办法规定限仓与强平等机制，交割办法限制自然人的交割持仓。具体品种条款与最新通知优先于概括性的时间表。涨跌停板限制可申报的价格，并不保证平仓委托能成交。

For a concrete distinction, the SHFE rebar specifications set a trading lot of 10 tonnes but a delivery unit of 300 tonnes. Trading one lot does not make one lot independently deliverable. The exchange’s trading guide explains same-day opening and closing; its older numerical schedules should not replace current product notices.一个具体区别是：上期所螺纹钢业务细则规定交易单位为10吨/手，交割单位却是300吨。可以交易一手，不代表这一手能够单独交割。交易所的合规交易指南解释了当日开平仓机制；其中较早的数值标准不能代替当前品种通知。

Calendar spreads: specify which difference you trade跨期套利：先把价差方向写对

Different delivery months respond to different inventory and supply conditions. A near-term shortage may support the nearby contract while expected new supply restrains a later one. A calendar spread trades this relative movement. Unlike the matched delivery identity above, two contracts expiring on different dates have no obligation to finish at the same price. CME’s spread overview distinguishes calendar, intercommodity and processing spreads.不同交割月份面对不同的库存和供给条件。近期缺货可能支撑近月，未来增产预期则可能压制远月。跨期组合交易的是这种相对变化。与前面的交割等式不同，两个到期日不同的合约，没有最终价格必须相等的约束。CME价差交易介绍区分了跨期、跨品种与产业链组合。

Buy 10 tonnes of nearby exposure and sell 10 tonnes of deferred exposure, then close both at the same later time. Nearby closes at 4,050 and deferred at 4,110.另取一个独立算例，报价单位仍为元/吨：近月4,000，远月4,100。约定D＝远月−近月。买入10吨近月敞口、卖出10吨远月敞口，之后在同一时点平掉两腿，平仓价格分别为近月4,050、远月4,110。

A model becomes a strategy only after execution and funding把模型变成策略，还缺成交与资金约束

For the two-leg calendar example, net realized trading P&L is the sum of each leg’s multiplier-adjusted price change minus complete trading costs. Compute it from actual contract prices and fills. A back-adjusted continuous series is useful for research, but its artificial roll adjustments must not be booked as tradable profit.对前面的两腿跨期组合，实际交易净盈亏等于两腿按乘数换算后的价格损益之和，再减去完整交易成本。应从真实月份合约价格与成交记录计算。复权连续行情可以辅助研究，但换月时人为加入的调整，不能记为可交易利润。

Signal timing信号时点
A signal calculated from a closing price can trade only after that information exists. Use subsequent executable quotes and account for latency.用收盘价算出的信号，只能在该信息形成之后交易。使用后续可成交报价，并考虑决策与下单延迟。
Two-leg execution两腿成交
A filled first leg leaves directional exposure until the second fills. Model partial fills, rejected orders and a time limit for completing or unwinding the pair.第一腿成交、第二腿未成交时，会留下单边敞口。需要模拟部分成交、拒单，以及补齐另一腿，或平掉已成交持仓的时限。
Costs完整成本
Include both legs’ opening and closing fees, bid–ask spread, impact, and relevant funding. If fills already use bid/ask prices, do not add the same spread again.计入两腿开平手续费、买卖价差、冲击成本与适用资金成本。若成交价已采用买一卖一，不要把同一项买卖价差重复计入成本。
Capital资金安排
Track daily equity, required margin, available cash and the deadline for replenishment. Stress higher margin rates and prices moving against both legs’ intended relative position.逐日记录权益、所需保证金、可用现金与补款期限。压测保证金提高，以及价差继续向不利方向变化的情况。
Expiry and exit到期与退出
Specify a last holding date, roll costs and model-invalidation conditions. Price limits or missing liquidity can prevent a stop from executing.明确最晚持仓日、换月成本与模型失效条件。涨跌停或流动性不足可能使止损委托无法成交。
Independent evaluation独立评价
Use time-ordered development and test periods. Report net return on committed capital, drawdown, peak cash need, turnover and performance by market regime.按时间划分开发与测试区间。报告相对于分配资本的净收益、最大回撤、峰值资金需求、换手，以及不同市场状态下的表现。

For a physical cash-and-carry trade, capital includes inventory funding as well as margin and liquidity reserves. For a futures-only spread, add both legs’ collateral needs under the actual offset rules and a cash buffer. Report the holding period alongside any return; profit divided by the smallest opening margin can make a capital-intensive trade look misleadingly attractive.实物期现套利需要现货资金、保证金和流动性准备；纯期货组合则要按真实抵扣规则核算两腿保证金，并准备现金缓冲。报告收益率时同时交代持有期限。用利润除以开仓时最低保证金，会把占用大量资金的交易包装成很高的回报。

Three checks before trusting an arbitrage claim用三个问题检验是否真正理解

Futures trade above spot. Is that enough?期货比现货贵，就能套利吗？

No. In the opening example the gross difference is CNY 600. If complete costs rise from 300 to 650, the same price pair loses CNY 50. Delivery compatibility and executable prices must be checked before the subtraction is meaningful.不能。开头算例的毛价差为600元，若完整成本从300元升至650元，同样的价格组合会亏损50元。在相减之前，还必须确认交割匹配与报价可成交。

Both months rise by CNY 50 per tonne. What happens?远近月都上涨50元/吨，组合赚多少？

For the earlier long-nearby/short-deferred position of 10 tonnes per leg, one leg earns CNY 500 and the other loses CNY 500. Gross P&L is zero; with CNY 40 of complete round-trip trading costs, net P&L is −40.对前面两腿各10吨的买近卖远组合，一腿赚500元，另一腿亏500元。毛利为零；若两腿完整往返交易成本为40元，净亏损就是40元。

A spread is three standard deviations from its mean. Must it revert?价差偏离均值三个标准差，一定会回归吗？

No. The mean or hedge coefficient may have changed, the sample may understate tail risk, or expiry may arrive first. Recheck the economic mechanism, the information available at the signal time and the funding needed if the deviation widens.不一定。均值或对冲系数可能已经改变，样本可能低估尾部风险，也可能先到持仓期限。需要重新检查经济机制、信号时刻可获得的信息，以及偏离继续扩大时的资金需求。

The opening firm earns the assumed CNY 300 only if the matching sale and complete cost assumptions hold through delivery. The CNY 540 shortfall is a different calculation: cash needed at an intermediate settlement. Evaluate every proposed arbitrage on both dimensions—what ties the prices together, and how the position survives until that relationship can be realized.开头那家企业能否得到假设的300元，取决于匹配销售与完整成本的条件能否一直成立到交割。540元缺口回答的是另一件事：中途某次结算需要补多少现金。评价一套套利模型，需要同时查明两点：什么机制约束价格关系，以及资金如何支持持仓等到这种关系兑现。
