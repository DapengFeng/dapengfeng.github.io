Put the target beside the limits先把目标与限制放在一起

An extra 25% of usable drive power sounds like an obvious upgrade. In the example below, it does not shorten the launch at all: the tyres are already the limiting element. A larger reduction ratio has the same result. The useful design question is which limit must move first, and what becomes the next constraint.可用电驱功率增加 25%，看起来是很直接的升级。但在下面的示例中，它完全没有缩短起步用时：轮胎已经先到极限。加大减速比，结果也一样。真正需要回答的是：先移动哪一条限制，之后又会被什么卡住。

Start with the target from Part 1’s launch calculation: rest to 60 mph in one second, without rollout. For this installment, choose an illustrative 2,000 kg vehicle. Specify constant acceleration for this target: the required forward force is 53.6 kN, from mass times acceleration. Before selecting a battery or motor, compare that requirement with the force each part of the system can support.从第一期的起步目标继续：从静止到 60 mph，用时一秒，不扣除起步距离。本期取一辆假设质量为 2,000 kg 的车。先规定这条目标轨迹采用匀加速：质量乘以加速度，得到所需前向力 53.6 kN。挑电池和电机之前，先把这项要求与系统各环节能支持的力放在一起。

Tesla’s Roadster reservation agreement, checked 18 October 2026, still describes a vehicle under development. The calculations do not presume its motor type, pack voltage, cell layout or gear ratios.下文硬件数值均为明确给定的模型假设，不是 Roadster 规格或实测结果。2026-10-18 核对的 Tesla Roadster 预订协议仍将车辆描述为开发中；本文不预设它的电机类型、电池电压、电芯排列或减速比。

The same model gives a useful counterexample: a gear change alone saves no time, but adding constant downforce and then changing the ratio cuts the idealized time from 1.823 to 1.464 s. The lowest ceiling decides真正起作用的是最低的那条上限

Use one baseline throughout: 2,000 kg, tyre coefficient μ = 1.5, aggregate motor torque 1,200 N·m, reduction ratio G = 9, rolling radius 0.34 m and 903.07 kW available wheel power. Assume all-wheel drive can use the whole-vehicle grip limit, with no additional downforce. Torque sets one force ceiling, grip another; the power ceiling falls with speed because force equals power divided by speed. The car can use only the lowest of the three.先固定同一组基线：质量 2,000 kg，轮胎附着系数 μ = 1.5，等效电机总转矩 1,200 N·m，减速比 G = 9，滚动半径 0.34 m，可用轮端功率 903.07 kW；假设全轮驱动能够利用整车抓地力，没有额外下压力。转矩和抓地力分别给出一条力的上限；功率对应的力则随车速下降，因为力等于功率除以速度。车真正能用上的，是三者中最低的一项。

The target requires 53.6 kN throughout the interval; the baseline can use only 29.43 kN. At 60 mph, the torque and power ceilings are also below the target, at 30.81 and 33.67 kN. Raising grip alone therefore cannot meet this trajectory either. Follow the power, including what becomes heat沿功率往前走，也追踪变成热的部分

The battery supplies DC electrical power. The inverter switches that supply to control motor winding currents; the motor turns electrical input into shaft torque and rotation. A reduction gear trades shaft speed for wheel torque. This division of work is described in the U.S. Department of Energy’s electric-vehicle component overview. Every stage has a limit, and every lossy stage leaves less power for the next one.电池提供直流电功率，逆变器通过开关控制电机绕组中的电流，电机把电能转换成轴上的转矩与转动，减速器再用转速换取轮端转矩。美国能源部的纯电动车部件说明给出了这些部件的分工。每一段都有自己的上限，存在损耗的环节也会让下一段能拿到的功率减少。

Voltage is measured in volts, current in amperes, torque in N·m and angular speed in rad/s. The efficiency factors are fractions of power retained. Real efficiencies vary with current, speed and temperature; fixed values here keep the accounting visible. They are unsuitable for predicting a whole drive cycle or standstill heating.电压用伏特，电流用安培，转矩用 N·m，角速度用 rad/s；各个效率系数表示保留下来的功率比例。真实效率随电流、转速和温度变化，这里固定取值，是为了看清功率如何分配，不能用来预测完整工况的能耗或堵转发热。

The battery has to hold its voltage under load电池必须在大电流下撑住电压

A pack that stores enough energy may still fail the power requirement. Drawing current lowers its terminal voltage and generates heat. A simple open-circuit voltage plus resistance model makes the coupling explicit; MathWorks’ battery equivalent-circuit documentation shows the fuller model, including dynamic polarization and additional heat terms.总能量足够，电池仍可能过不了功率这一关。电流流出时，端电压会下降，也会产生热。用开路电压加内阻的简化模型，就能看出两者如何耦合；MathWorks 电池等效电路文档还包含动态极化及其他热量项。

Here resistance is an approximation for a specified state and timescale, not a permanent pack constant. State of charge, temperature, minimum cell voltage, current limits and pulse duration all matter. A useful specification therefore says how much power is available, for how long, and from which starting conditions. The Battery Power Estimator equations explicitly combine voltage, current, state of charge and a prediction interval.这里的内阻只是特定状态和时间尺度下的近似，并非电池包永远不变的常数。荷电状态、温度、最低单体电压、电流上限和脉冲持续时间都会影响结果。因此，有用的规格必须说明：从什么初始状态出发，能提供多大功率，持续多久。电池可用功率估计器的公式就同时考虑了电压、电流、荷电状态和预测时间窗口。

Higher voltage does not erase cell heating提高电压，并没有消除电芯发热

At fixed power, doubling voltage halves pack current. A cable with unchanged resistance then produces one quarter of the resistive heat. But rearrange the same cells into twice as many series positions and half as many parallel branches: pack resistance becomes four times larger. Each cell still carries the same current. The cable benefit and the cell result are different calculations.相同功率下，电压翻倍，整包电流减半；如果一根电缆的电阻不变，它的电阻发热就降到四分之一。但若只是把同一批电芯改成两倍串联、半数并联，整包内阻会变成四倍，每颗电芯承受的电流仍然相同。电缆得到的好处，与电芯内部的结果，是两笔不同的账。

Current makes torque; voltage makes room for speed电流建立转矩，电压为转速留下余量

The inverter does more than turn DC into AC. By switching the DC bus and using current feedback, it controls the magnitude and direction of the stator’s magnetic field relative to the rotor. That relationship produces torque. In a permanent-magnet motor example, field-oriented control separates a torque-producing current component from a flux-related component. Rotor saliency in an interior-permanent-magnet design also contributes reluctance torque. None of this identifies which motor Tesla will use.逆变器不只是把直流变成交流。它切换直流母线电压，并利用电流反馈，控制定子磁场相对转子的大小与方向，由这种关系产生转矩。以永磁电机为例，磁场定向控制把电流分成与转矩、磁通相关的分量；内嵌式永磁电机的转子磁阻在不同方向上存在差异，还能由此产生磁阻转矩。这里解释一种电机原理，不据此判断 Tesla 将使用哪种电机。

At low speed, winding current, inverter current and heat typically constrain torque. As speed rises, the motor’s back EMF rises too, leaving less voltage headroom for current control. Beyond a base speed, field weakening reduces the effective flux to keep operating within the voltage limit, while consuming part of the available current capability. TI’s IPMSM field-weakening and MTPA application note, sections 2.2–2.3, develops these current and voltage constraints.低速时，绕组电流、逆变器电流能力和发热通常限制了转矩。转速升高后，电机反电动势也随之增大，留给电流控制的电压余量变小。超过基速后，弱磁控制降低有效磁通，使运行仍满足电压限制，同时占用一部分可用电流能力。TI 的 IPMSM 弱磁与 MTPA 应用笔记第 2.2—2.3 节展开了这些电流与电压约束。

Battery current and motor phase current are not interchangeable. At standstill, a motor can produce torque while mechanical power is zero because angular speed is zero. Winding current and heat can still be substantial. Dividing mechanical power by pack voltage cannot recover that phase current.电池直流电流不能直接当作电机相电流。静止时，电机可以产生转矩，但角速度为零，机械功率也为零；绕组里仍可能有很大的电流与发热。拿机械功率除以电池电压，算不出这个相电流。

For the next calculation, replace these detailed limits with a constant-torque region followed by a constant-power region, ending at a maximum mechanical speed. It is a useful envelope, not a complete motor map. Real high-speed power can fall, and temperature or bus-voltage changes can move the boundaries.为了继续计算，下面把这些复杂限制近似成：先恒转矩，后恒功率，最后受最高机械转速限制。这个包络适合分析，但不是完整电机地图。真实电机在高转速下可能继续掉功率，温度和母线电压变化也会移动这些边界。
