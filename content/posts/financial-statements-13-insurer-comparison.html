Insurance liabilities carry promises that survive the current reporting period. Under IFRS 17, the contractual service margin (CSM) represents unearned profit recognized as future insurance services are provided. It is not a pool of freely withdrawable cash. China’s new insurance-contract accounting uses the corresponding service-based recognition logic; the accounting basis of each filing remains the reference. IFRS Foundation: IFRS 17 principles.保险负债承载的承诺，不会随当期报表结账而消失。IFRS 17 下的合同服务边际（CSM），是随未来保险服务提供逐步确认的未赚利润，不是一池可以直接提走的现金。中国新保险合同准则采用相应的按服务确认逻辑，具体仍应以各份披露的会计口径为准。IFRS 基金会：IFRS 17 原则说明。

This changes the owner-earnings exercise from the Apple chapters. Subtracting capex from an insurer’s operating cash flow leaves policyholder obligations and capital needs unresolved. Operating profit removes specified accounting volatility using management assumptions, but is not cash either. A high solvency ratio measures capital relative to required capital; it does not say that the excess percentage can be distributed.这改变了前面 Apple 案例中所有者盈余的分析方式。保险公司的经营现金流减去资本支出后，保单义务和资本需求仍未得到处理。营运利润按管理层规定的假设剔除部分波动，也不是现金。较高的偿付能力充足率表示资本相对最低资本更充足，不表示超出的百分比都可以用于分红。

Business conclusions and valuation conditions经营结论与估值条件

The clearest operating finding is improving P&C underwriting alongside diverging life new-business trends. All five groups reported lower attributable profit in the first nine months of 2026 than a year earlier, but that does not establish a shared deterioration in their insurance operations. Nor does a profit decline alone make a share cheap. The business comparison above separates the evidence by segment.最明确的经营结论是：财险承保在改善，寿险新业务价值的走势则出现分化。五家公司 2026 年前三季度归母利润都同比下降，但这不足以证明它们的保险业务同时恶化。利润回落本身，也不能证明股票便宜。前文的业务对比按业务范围列出证据，下面据此形成判断。

Estimate sustainable shareholder returns估计可持续股东回报

The economic attraction of insurance depends on the cost of its obligations and the returns earned against them. Premium growth creates obligations as well as investable funds. First estimate sustainable underwriting or life-policy earnings under realistic assumptions for claims, guarantees and expenses. Then reconcile necessary capital retention and cash upstreaming before estimating what can reach the listed parent and its shareholders. Do not deduct costs already included in earnings a second time, or treat the entire required-capital balance as an annual expense. The parent-cash reconciliation above makes this last step observable.保险生意的吸引力，取决于承担负债的成本以及与之匹配的资产回报。保费增长既带来可投资资金，也增加未来义务。先在合理的赔付、保证成本和费用假设下估计可持续的承保收益或寿险保单经济收益，再核对必要资本留存与现金上划，判断能有多少钱回到上市母公司及其股东。已计入收益的成本不能再次扣减，也不能把必要资本的全部余额当成每年的费用。前文的母公司现金勾稽就是最后一步的具体检验。

The entire 2025 profit uplift cannot be assumed to recur each year. Valuation should use conservative investment returns and capital needs, check actual subsidiary distributions, and express the resulting range per share. Retained earnings deserve a higher valuation only when their reinvestment can produce adequate additional value. A high dividend yield without a sustainable source of cash does not pass this test.2025 年的全部利润增量，不能一概视为每年可以重复的收益。估值应采用保守的投资回报与资本需求，结合子公司实际分红上划，形成每股可分配收益的区间。留存利润只有在再投入能够创造足够新增价值时，才值得给予相应估值。只有较高股息率，却解释不清现金来源，仍不满足这一判断条件。

Test how the investment thesis could fail检验判断的失败条件

Lower reinvestment returns can leave insufficient income to meet policy guarantees; unexpectedly large claims can reveal inadequate reserves; subsidiary capital needs can interrupt cash upstreaming. These mechanisms can occur together. An incentive tied heavily to premium volume or current profit is therefore a reason to examine pricing, persistency and reserve development. It is not evidence that any of these five companies has misstated its accounts.再投资收益降低，可能使投资收益不足以覆盖保单保证成本；赔付超出预期，可能暴露准备金不足；子公司需要补充资本，可能导致分红上划受限。这些问题还可能同时发生。因此，若考核过度依赖保费规模或当期利润，就应进一步检查定价、持续率及准备金发展。这是待检验的机制，不是在指认五家公司存在会计失实。

Sustainable insurance economics require adequate prices and reserves across the portfolio. Persistent non-motor losses or later reserve strengthening that consumes today’s gains would weaken this judgment. Valuing the listed group also requires minority interests and its other businesses.承保改善有直接证据，车险盈利不能证明其他险种具有同样的承保质量。2025 年车险承保利润高于财险整体利润；2026 年前三季度财险承保利润增长 11.3%，集团利润却下降。保险业务能否持续创造价值，仍取决于各险种定价与准备金是否充分。如果非车险持续亏损，或后续准备金补提消耗了今天的收益，就应下调这一判断。估值中国人保集团时，还必须处理少数股东权益与其他业务。

The decision now: proceed to valuation, with explicit conditions现在的行动结论：带着条件进入估值

For the next stage, prioritize the quality of the available evidence: use P&C underwriting as a direct operating comparison; value life businesses under conservative guarantees and reinvestment assumptions; reconcile cash upstreaming and uses for conglomerates. The provisional judgments above define what must hold and what would change the conclusion. They do not establish a durable moat or a risk-adjusted ranking from two full years and a nine-month update.下一步应按证据的可验证程度推进：先用财险承保做直接的经营对照，对寿险采用保守的保证成本与再投资假设，对综合集团核对现金上划及用途。上面五条判断明确了哪些条件需要成立、什么变化会推翻结论。两份完整年报加一期前三季度更新，还不足以证明持久护城河或形成风险调整后的统一排名。

A purchase decision additionally needs a same-date A- or H-share price, the matching share-count basis, and a conservative per-share value range that incorporates capital needs and downside scenarios. Consider a purchase only if the price leaves room for estimation error and the key obligations can be understood. If the apparent bargain disappears when exceptional investment gains are normalized or guarantees are stressed, wait rather than use a low headline P/E as a substitute for the missing evidence. That is the practical output of this comparison: a research decision and its rejection conditions, with valuation still to complete.买入判断还需要同一时点的 A 股或 H 股价格、匹配的股数口径，以及纳入资本需求和不利情景的保守每股价值区间。只有关键负债能够理解，价格也为估计误差留出了余地，才进入买入讨论。如果所谓便宜在剔除不可重复的投资收益、对保证成本施压后就消失，应暂缓判断，不能用较低的表面市盈率代替缺失的证据。这次比较已经形成研究方向与否决条件，估值工作仍需继续完成。

An exercise: reject the wrong comparison, then make a useful one练习：先排除错误比较，再形成有效判断

Three claims in a draft research note研究笔记中的三句话

A draft says: “CPIC earned 535.05 亿元 in 2025 and 364.03 亿元 in 2026, so annual earnings fell 32%. Ping An’s lower combined ratio proves it is the best insurer. New China Life’s 3.5% investment yield beats CPIC’s 2.5%.” Correct all three statements, then write one defensible comparative conclusion.一份笔记写道：“太保 2025 年赚 535.05 亿元，2026 年赚 364.03 亿元，全年盈利下降约 32%；平安综合成本率更低，说明它是最好的保险公司；新华 3.5% 的投资收益率胜过太保的 2.5%。”请逐句纠正，再写出一条有依据的比较结论。

Check the reasoning核对推理

First, 364.03 covers only nine months; compare it with 457.00, giving a 20.3% decline. Second, the lower reported combined ratio applies to P&C underwriting on the stated scope, not the entire group; product mix and reserves still need investigation. Third, one yield is annualized and the other is not. A supported conclusion is: all three P&C businesses improved their own reported ratios, while life NBV trends diverged in 2026. The next research step is to explain these changes within each scope.第一，364.03 只覆盖九个月，应与同期 457.00 比较，降幅为 20.3%。第二，较低的披露成本率描述的是特定范围内的财险承保，不代表整个集团，仍需检查险种结构与准备金。第三，一个收益率已年化，另一个未年化。可以成立的结论是：三家财险业务各自的披露成本率都改善，而 2026 年寿险 NBV 的走势出现分化。下一步是在各自口径内解释这些变化。
