Start with the monthly saving先算每月能省多少钱

You already pay an API provider to run a model. Buying servers changes the bill: you pay for hardware now, then electricity, operations and any API traffic the servers cannot handle. The decision is whether those future savings can repay the purchase before the equipment stops being useful.你现在每月付钱调用模型 API。如果改成买机器自己跑，就要先付设备款，以后再付电费、运维费，以及机器处理不了时的 API 费。要判断的事情很具体：以后每月省下来的钱，能不能在设备失去使用价值之前，把买机器的钱挣回来。

Compare the same completed work at acceptable quality and latency. Then write down three amounts: the upfront investment I, the monthly all-API bill A, and the complete monthly cost of the local route L. The monthly saving is S:先保证两种方案能以合格的质量和速度，完成同样的工作。然后记下三个数：一次性投入 I、全部使用 API 的月账单 A、改成本地方案后的完整月运行费用 L。每月省下的钱就是 S：

L includes local operations and fallback API charges. Hardware bought at the start belongs in I; do not also charge its depreciation as a monthly cash payment. If S stays at or below zero, running locally will not recover the hardware outlay through operating savings. If S is positive, the next question is how long that saving lasts.L 要包含本地运行费和回退 API 费。开始时买设备的钱计入 I，不要再把折旧当成每月真实付款重复扣除。如果 S 一直不大于零，本地运行就无法靠省下的账单收回设备款；如果 S 为正，还要看这笔节省能持续多久。

First, calculate the API bill from tokens先用 token 算出 API 账单

A total token count is not enough to price a workload: ordinary input, cached input and output can have different rates. To see the difference, assume prices of CNY 2, 0.5 and 8 per million tokens, respectively. These are illustrative rates, not a provider quote. Hold monthly input plus output at 100 million tokens, then change their proportions.知道总共用了多少 token，还不能直接得到账单：普通输入、缓存输入和输出的单价可能不同。先假设三者每百万 token 分别收费 2 元、0.5 元和 8 元。这些是便于计算的假设单价，不是供应商报价。下面固定每月输入加输出共 1 亿 token，只改变两者比例。

Take 90 million input tokens and 10 million output tokens. With no cache hits, the bill is CNY 260. If 80% of the input hits the cache, 72 million input tokens use the cached rate and the remaining 18 million use the ordinary rate. Output is still 10 million tokens, so the bill falls to CNY 152:先算一组：9,000 万输入、1,000 万输出。没有缓存命中时，账单是 260 元。如果输入中有 80% 命中缓存，就是 7,200 万按缓存价计费，剩下 1,800 万按普通输入价计费；输出仍是 1,000 万，账单降为 152 元：

At the same 100-million-token total, the uncached bills are CNY 260, 500 and 740. With 80% input-cache hits they become CNY 152, 440 and 728. In this example, output is four times the ordinary input price: output-heavy usage costs more, and input caching has little room to help it. An input-heavy document analysis and an output-heavy generation job may resemble these mixes, but equal token totals do not mean equal work or quality.同样是 1 亿 token，无缓存时三种账单分别为 260、500、740 元；输入缓存命中 80% 后，分别为 152、440、728 元。这组单价里，输出是普通输入的四倍，因此输出越多，账单越高，输入缓存能省下的部分也越少。长文档分析可能偏重输入，长内容生成可能偏重输出；但相同 token 总量并不代表完成了相同工作，也不代表相同质量。

Follow one purchase through the numbers用一笔账看懂回本、ROI 和 NPV

Keep those illustrative prices and consider a larger monthly workload: 3 billion input tokens and 750 million output tokens, with no cache hits. The API bill is 3,000 × 2 + 750 × 8 = CNY 12,000. This supplies A in the investment calculation.沿用这组假设单价，把月用量扩大到30 亿输入、7.5 亿输出，缓存命中为零。API 账单就是 3,000 × 2 + 750 × 8 = 12,000 元。这样，投资计算里的 A 就有了来处。

Now assume an upfront purchase and setup cost of CNY 100,000, and CNY 5,000 a month in local operating costs including fallback, while completing that same work at acceptable quality and latency. Use a 36-month horizon, constant monthly costs, month-end settlement and no resale proceeds. These are teaching assumptions, not a hardware quote or a measured capacity claim. Token billing alone does not establish how much local hardware the workload needs.再假设买设备并上线共花 10 万元，能够以合格的质量和速度完成同一批工作，电费、运维和回退 API 合计每月 5,000 元。计划使用 36 个月，期间费用不变，均在月末结算，期末不计卖设备的钱。这是一组教学假设，不是设备报价，也不证明某台机器能承接上述负载。仅靠 token 账单，还不能推出本地需要多少硬件。

Payback: when does the CNY 100,000 come back?回本周期：多久挣回这 10 万元？

CNY 100,000 divided by CNY 7,000 is about 14.3 months. With savings received at month end, recovery occurs in month 15: after 14 months you are still CNY 2,000 short; after 15, you are CNY 5,000 ahead. If the hardware must be replaced in month 12, this purchase has not paid back by then.10 万元除以每月 7,000 元，约为 14.3 个月。按月末收到节省的现金，第 15 个月回本：第 14 个月还差 2,000 元，第 15 个月已经多出 5,000 元。如果设备第 12 个月就必须换掉，这笔采购在那之前还没有回本。

This shortcut requires a constant positive saving. When usage or prices change, add the actual monthly savings in order and find the first month that covers the outlay. A later return to a negative balance matters too.这个除法只适用于每月节省固定且为正的情况。用量或价格变化时，就逐月累加实际节省，找出第一次覆盖初投的月份；此后余额若再次转负，也要一并看见。

ROI: how much net saving does the investment produce?ROI：这笔投入最终多省下多少？

The CNY 100,000 investment produces CNY 152,000 of net saving over three years. That is a 36-month ROI of 152%, not a 152% annual return. ROI summarizes the total; it does not tell you when the money comes back.投入 10 万元，三年净省 15.2 万元，所以36 个月 ROI 为 152%，不是每年赚 152%。ROI 说明总回报有多少，但不会告诉你资金什么时候回来。

NPV: is the future saving worth the money paid today?NPV：未来省的钱，值得今天先付吗？

Money available today has other uses. Choose a discount rate that reflects the project’s funding and risk assumptions, then express each future saving in today’s money. With an assumed annual rate of 10%, the 36 monthly savings are worth about CNY 218,305 today. Subtract the CNY 100,000 purchase and the net present value is CNY 118,305.今天手里的钱还有别的用途。选定与项目资金成本、风险假设相匹配的折现率，把未来每笔节省折算成今天的钱。假设年折现率为 10%，这 36 笔月节省合计相当于今天的约 21.83 万元；扣掉今天付出的 10 万元，净现值 NPV 约为 11.83 万元。

Positive NPV means this purchase beats the all-API alternative under the chosen assumptions and discount rate. It does not establish that buying beats every rental offer. Use payback for the cash-recovery deadline, ROI for total return, and NPV for value after accounting for timing.NPV 为正，表示在这些假设和折现率下，这次购机比继续全部使用 API 更划算；它还不能证明购机胜过所有租赁报价。回本周期看资金要等多久，ROI 看总回报，NPV 看考虑时间之后是否仍值得投入。

What would change the decision?什么情况下应该改选方案

If monthly savings remain negative, keep the API route on cost grounds. Local deployment then costs more to operate before paying back any hardware. Privacy or offline requirements may still justify it, but that is a different reason to spend.如果每月节省持续为负，单从成本上应继续用 API。这意味着设备款还没开始回收，本地运行本身就更贵。隐私或离线要求仍可能使你必须自建，但那是另一项购买理由。

If monthly savings are positive but payback is too late, test rental or a smaller purchase. A 30-month payback is weak support for equipment you expect to replace after 18 months. Compare complete rental cost for the same service, not only the advertised GPU-hour price.如果每月省钱，但回本太晚，先比较租赁或缩小采购。预计 18 个月就要替换的设备，很难靠 30 个月的回本计划说服人。租赁也要比较同等服务的完整费用，不能只看宣传中的 GPU 小时单价。

If payback fits the useful life and NPV is positive, test the assumptions that could overturn it. Reduce demand, lower API prices and raise operating costs separately. If a small plausible change reverses the decision, a short paid trial is more useful than a large purchase.如果回本早于使用寿命，而且 NPV 为正，再检验它有多稳。分别降低需求、降低 API 单价、提高运行费。如果一个合理的小变化就会使结论翻转，先做小规模付费试验，比直接大额采购更有用。

For each candidate, save its own measured token volumes and throughput. Different tokenizers, reasoning lengths and retry rates can change the work billed. How tokens become the API billtoken 怎样变成 API 账单

For each time period, let Nin and Nout be billed token counts; h is the cached-input fraction. The rates phit, pmiss and pout are per million tokens. Add the bills of all periods to get monthly A.每个时段的计费 token 数记为 Nin、Nout，输入缓存命中比例为 h；phit、pmiss、pout 是每百万 token 的价格。各时段费用相加，就是每月的 A。

Include repeated context and retries in billed usage, and count reasoning output once. With flat pricing, both period rates are identical. Missing cache prices do not mean free caching. Extra tool, audio or image charges need separate cash entries.计费用量包含重复上下文与重试，思考输出不能重复计算。不分时计费时，两组单价相同。缺少缓存单价不表示缓存免费；工具、音频或图像的额外费用需要另行计入现金流。

How capacity determines fallback API traffic怎样算机器处理不了、仍需付 API 费的部分

Let Δb be seconds in a time bucket, u its scheduling headroom factor, a availability, and qin/qout jointly measured token rates. For that bucket’s demand, the local fraction f is bounded by the first exhausted capacity:Δb 是一个时段的秒数，u 是扣除突发余量后可使用的比例，a 是可用率，qin／qout 是同时测得的 token 速率。本地承接比例 f 取决于哪项能力先用尽：

Route fraction f locally and 1−f to the API, keeping the input and output of the workload together. Ignore a ratio whose demand is zero. Multiply fallback tokens by their own cache-aware rates, then add that bill to local running costs to obtain L.其中 f 的负载由本地承担，1−f 回到 API，输入与输出按同一比例分流。需求为零的维度忽略对应比值。回退 token 按它们自己的缓存命中率计费，再加到本地运行费里，就得到 L。

This is a planning approximation with uniform arrivals inside each period and outages independent of demand. It does not simulate queues. Long-context routing or bursty traffic requires a workload replay; separate peak input and output benchmarks cannot be combined into one operating point. Do not deduct availability or headroom twice if measurements already include them.这是时段内均匀到达、停机与需求独立的规划近似，不模拟排队。长上下文分流或明显突发流量需要用真实负载重放验证；不能把分别测出的输入峰值、输出峰值拼在一起。实测已包含的可用率或余量，不再重复扣除。

Which costs belong in the comparison?哪些成本容易漏算或重复算

The upfront amount includes accelerators, host memory, storage, networking, installation and migration that differ from the API route. Running costs include electricity, hosting, network service, maintenance and added labor. Electricity can start with average IT kW × hours × PUE × electricity price; do not add cooling again when the hosting bill already includes it.初投包含相对 API 方案新增的加速器、主机内存、存储、网络、安装和迁移。运行费包含电费、托管、网络、维护与新增人力。电费可以从“平均 IT 功率 × 小时数 × PUE × 电价”估算，托管已包含制冷时不要再算一遍。

Only costs identical across alternatives cancel. For rental, compare a complete service at the same quality, latency and availability. If data cannot leave your environment, all-API and public-API fallback are not feasible alternatives. For a later keep-or-sell decision, compare future cash and the sale proceeds forgone by holding; the old purchase is not a new payment.只有各方案完全相同的费用才能抵消。租赁要按相同质量、延迟和可用率比较完整服务。如果数据不能外发，全 API 和公网 API 回退就不是可行方案。设备买下后再决定留用还是出售，应比较未来现金与放弃的出售所得，历史购机款不能再当成一次新付款。
