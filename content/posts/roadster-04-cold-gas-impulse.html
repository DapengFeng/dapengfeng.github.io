Specify the whole second先把这一秒定义完整

Ask for one precise contribution: an extra 10 kN of forward thrust for one second. This requires 10 kN·s of impulse. On a 2,000 kg vehicle, ignoring added hardware, changing mass and other forces, that impulse contributes about 5 m/s of velocity. It does not by itself establish a one-second 0–60 mph time. The missing quantity in a peak-thrust claim is how long the force remains available.把任务限定清楚：在一秒内，持续向车身额外提供 10 kN 向前推力。这需要 10 kN·s 的冲量。对一辆 2,000 kg 的车，暂不计新增硬件、质量变化与其他受力，这份冲量约能贡献 5 m/s 的速度增量；它本身不能证明一秒完成 0–60 mph。峰值推力之外，还缺一项决定性的条件：这股力能维持多久。

The tyres in Part 3 exchange momentum with the road. A cold-gas jet exchanges momentum with expelled gas, so it can add force without asking the tyre contact patches to transmit that force. Start with momentum; keep the pressure term从动量守恒开始，把压力项留下

A cold-gas system does not burn fuel in the nozzle. Compressed gas accelerates as it expands: its enthalpy supplies the jet’s kinetic energy. In a control volume around a quasi-steady nozzle, the outgoing momentum flux produces one part of the reaction force. If exit pressure exceeds ambient pressure, the pressure difference supplies another. NASA’s thrust balance keeps both terms.冷气系统不在喷嘴中燃烧燃料。压缩气体在膨胀中加速，气体的焓转化为喷流动能。对一个准稳态喷嘴写控制体的动量平衡，流出动量产生一部分反作用力；出口压力若高于环境压力，压差还会贡献另一部分。NASA 的推力方程保留了这两项。

The energy balance of an adiabatic nozzle, with negligible inlet speed, is cₚT₀ = cₚTₑ + vₑ²/2. T₀ is the inlet stagnation temperature, Tₑ the exit static temperature, and cₚ the specific heat at constant pressure. Lower temperature means less energy available per kilogram to turn into jet speed.这里 ṁ 是每秒排出的质量，vₑ 是相对喷嘴的出口速度，Aₑ 是出口面积，pₑ 与 pₐ 分别为出口和环境压力。对入口速度可忽略的绝热喷嘴，能量平衡为 cₚT₀ = cₚTₑ + vₑ²/2。T₀ 是入口总温，Tₑ 是出口静温，cₚ 是定压比热。温度降低，意味着每千克气体能转化为喷流速度的能量减少。

Constant thrust still empties a cooling tank推力不变，储罐仍在降温

The tank wall does not move, but gas and energy leave it. Write the balance for a well-mixed, rigid, adiabatic tank: its internal energy is mcᵥT, where cᵥ is the specific heat at constant volume, while each kilogram leaving carries enthalpy cₚT. Let m be the gas remaining and ṁ > 0 the outflow. There is no wall work or heat transfer in this baseline.罐壁没有移动，气体和能量却不断流出。对充分混合、刚性、绝热的储罐写能量平衡：罐内内能为 mcᵥT，cᵥ 是定容比热，每千克流出的气体则带走焓 cₚT。m 表示剩余气体质量，ṁ > 0 表示流出量；基准模型中没有罐壁做功，也没有外界传热。

Expanding the derivative and using cₚ − cᵥ = R gives dT/T = (γ − 1)dm/m. Combining that result with pV = mRT yields two useful ratios, with subscript i marking the initial state. An ideal adiabatic regulator throttles without changing enthalpy; for our ideal gas, its outlet stagnation temperature follows the tank temperature. While p₀ stays at 3 MPa, cooling makes ṁ rise as 1/√T and vₑ fall as √T. Their product stays constant, as does the exit pressure contribution. The force remains 10 kN while gas consumption rises from 18.9 to 26.3 kg/s.理想绝热稳压阀按等焓节流处理；对本例理想气体，阀后总温跟随罐温下降。p₀ 保持 3 MPa 时，冷却使 ṁ 按 1/√T 增加，vₑ 按 √T 降低，乘积保持不变，出口压力项也不变。因此推力仍是 10 kN，耗气却从 18.9 kg/s 增至 26.3 kg/s。

Size storage from duration, not peak force先定持续时间，再反推储气空间

For fixed initial pressure, temperature and nozzle supply pressure, the regulated nozzle determines the consumption rate while tank volume determines the initial inventory. The resulting plateau duration scales linearly with volume. Under the adiabatic assumptions above, one second at 10 kN needs approximately 75.6 L of internal tank volume; two seconds need 151 L. Wall thickness, mounts, valves and clearances occupy additional space.初始压力、温度和喷嘴供压固定后，稳压喷嘴决定耗气速率，储罐容积决定初始存量，因此推力平台时长与容积成正比。在前述绝热假设下，10 kN 持续一秒约需 75.6 L 储罐内部容积，两秒约需 151 L；罐壁、支架、阀组和安装间隙还要占用额外空间。

Increasing throat area delivers force faster and spends gas faster. Raising storage pressure puts more gas in a given volume, but also changes vessel stress, material requirements and real-gas behaviour. Neither change removes the need to carry the gas and its containment. For this one-second task, a stable supply path and its flow capacity should be settled before chasing a larger peak.加大喉部会更快交付推力，也更快消耗气体；提高储气压力能在同一容积里装入更多气体，但也改变容器应力、材料要求与真实气体行为。两种改动都没有消除气体及其容器的携带代价。对这一秒的任务，应先确定稳定供气路径及其流量能力，再追求更高峰值。

Temperature recovery between pulses complicates repeated use. Gas warming against the vessel wall can make the gauge pressure rise without adding a single kilogram. The second pulse therefore needs the remaining mass and temperature, not just the pressure reading or an assumed repeat of the first pulse.多次使用还受脉冲间回温影响：气体从罐壁吸热后，即使一千克气体也没补进去，压力表也会回升。因此第二次脉冲需要知道剩余质量和温度，不能只看表压，更不能直接照搬第一次的推力曲线。

For a one-second auxiliary push, the first design question is whether the complete supply path can maintain the required nozzle pressure while consumption rises. Direct feed is simpler but accepts a decaying force; regulation holds a more useful plateau at the cost of flow capacity, additional components and pressure margin. A different nozzle expansion ratio may improve gas efficiency, but it must be compared with the same storage and installed-mass budget.对一秒辅助推进，首先要解决的是整条供气路径能否在耗气不断上升时，维持所需喷嘴入口压力。直接供气较简单，但必须接受推力衰减；稳压能保持更有用的平台，代价是更大的流量能力、额外部件与压差余量。换用更充分膨胀的喷嘴也许能提高气体利用效率，但必须放在相同储气与装车质量预算下比较。
